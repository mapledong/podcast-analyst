## Background

> Silicon Valley 101 reports from WWDC26 after two years of Apple AI disappointment: delayed features, lawsuits over advertised capabilities, and a 2025 'AI crisis' for a company wired for yearly big-bang releases that look glacial versus model cadence. Guests contrast Apple's distribution moat with the lack of a SOTA foundation model, and unpack why PCC on Google Cloud is both a privacy bet and a hardware-trust engineering problem.

---

## Key Facts

> **F1** 2024-06-10 Apple Intelligence was followed by slips; 2025 marketed undelivered AI and drew litigation. A secret early-2025 meeting chaired by then-COO Jeff Williams reframed failure as org/culture/leadership: John Giannandrea lost Cook's trust; Mike Rockwell (ex-Vision Pro) took Siri under Craig Federighi rather than becoming top AI chief; Amar Subramanya (16 years Google on Gemini/Bard, briefly Microsoft) joined as model lead; Giannandrea later left. Cook became hands-on on AI; Ternus inherits as CEO after September 2026.

> **F2** WWDC demos emphasize agentic Siri via App Intents: developers declare structured actions/data so Siri can tool-call into apps. Guests say Apple is early — third-party depth thin, US-first rollout likely. Private Cloud Compute aims for stateless encrypted inference with no retention for training/profiling; new PCC stacks Google Cloud hardware with Intel TDX-style confidential VMs so Apple Silicon security ideas travel off-device.

> **F3** Investor take: Cook's 15 years were a Wall Street honeymoon with fewer product fireworks; Ternus faces a 'return to product era.' Distribution and privacy remain assets; Gemini partnership plus Amar's hire buy time while Apple proves self-model competence.

## Key Insights

> **1.** Apple's AI crisis was organizational before model-sized.
>
> **Q** Why reorganize?
>
> **A** Yearly perfect-experience culture failed against weekly model shifts; Rockwell/Federighi/Subramanya redraw ownership so Siri ships inside software while models get a Gemini-hardened lead.

> **2.** PCC on Google Cloud exports Apple trust to TEEs.
>
> **Q** Is privacy theater?
>
> **A** Design is stateless: encrypt, compute, wipe. Hard part is cost/perf of confidential inference at global scale.

> **3.** App Intents is the quiet agent strategy.
>
> **Q** Where is the iPhone moment?
>
> **A** When Siri reliably tool-calls third-party apps end-to-end. Distribution is the edge; unfinished developer surface is the gap.
