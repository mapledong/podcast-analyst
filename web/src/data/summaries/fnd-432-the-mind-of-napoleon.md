## Key Facts

> **F1** Book and doctrine: first published 1955, ≈300 pages grouped by theme, Napoleon speaking. Chief doctrine, repeated: sacrifice comfort, self-interest, and happiness to destiny; destiny must be fulfilled. Work is his element — he has known the limits of his legs and eyes, never of working capacity. Inaction is unbearable. Luck is a consecutive series of great actions, the ability to exploit accidents; shallow men believe in luck, strong men in cause and effect (Emerson). Andreessen's malleable-world line from episode 50 is the modern rhyme. Immortality: to live without glory, without a trace, is not to have lived. Men of genius are meteors consumed lighting their century.

> **F2** Psychology and method: men are moved by two levers, fear and self-interest — the advertising founders (Lasker, Hopkins, Ogilvy) later sell the same way. Start by believing the worst; people would have to be exceptional rascals to match his assumption (Munger/Cicero cynicism; episode 286). His wife could have died and it would not have interrupted plans for a quarter of an hour. Suicide is a Monday-night mood that steals Saturday. 'It is success which makes the great man' — Britain had to recognize Washington because he won. All great events hang by a single thread; the less clever man neglects one thing and misses everything (LBJ: if you do everything you will win). Imagination rules the world. Senra's Readwise pile is 20,000+ highlights; he keeps tying this book to episode 214 (Isaacson/Jobs), 251 (Franklin/Washington, 250–300 pages), and 299 (Jobs archive).

> **F3** Propaganda and contradiction: 1806 he wants no book censorship; a footnote says in 1810 he established it. He informs the Pope he can depose him as predecessors did. 'The masses must be guided without their noticing it' — ink and paper. He dictated all important army bulletins to inform, counter rumor, mislead the enemy, and stir enthusiasm; he was his own minister of propaganda. When shaved he skipped French papers: they only say what he tells them. The art of ruling is making others work — then he dictates 21 large sheets for Josephine's water-cure itinerary. Jobs came back to Apple and sat a long Wednesday three-hour marketing meeting because great products still need a great marketing company; Senra's read of Insanely Simple is that no Missouri billboard shipped without Steve. Same instinct, different stakes.

## Mental Model · *Destiny Then Action × Own Propaganda × Dated Contradictions*

> **Components**
>
> First name the destiny, then spend the thought as a step to an act. Treat luck as exploited accidents. Move people with fear and self-interest, not product features. Success, not permission, makes the great man — Washington vs Britain. Do the communications yourself: bulletins, Wednesday marketing, every external page. Expect hypocrisy; Herold dates the quotes so 1806 no-censorship sits next to 1810 censorship. Micromanage the message; try not to dictate 21 sheets of spa itinerary. Study the man in his own words (294 for the life, 432 for the mind).
>
> **Application**
>
> AAPL is the Jobs rhyme Senra keeps pressing — own the marketing meeting, ship no billboard you have not seen. BRK.B is the Buffett/Munger shelf this tape keeps opening (opportunity-driven, cynicism about human nature, do not go where you will die). Private:Napoleon is the methods object, not a security. Fade founders who wait for luck or outsource the story. Do not copy unlimited-power information control as a product strategy; copy the 'do it yourself' part. A quote without a year is how you miss the contradiction.

## Key Insights

> **1.** Thought is only a step.
>
> **Q** What is the doctrine?
>
> **A** Find the destiny, then act. Work has no known limit. Inaction is the thing he cannot bear.

> **2.** Success makes the great man.
>
> **Q** Why did Britain recognize Washington?
>
> **A** Because he won. Permission follows the result. Imagination rules; plans stay as projects.

> **3.** Be your own minister of propaganda.
>
> **Q** Who writes the bulletin?
>
> **A** Napoleon dictated them. Jobs took Wednesday. French papers only said what he told them.
