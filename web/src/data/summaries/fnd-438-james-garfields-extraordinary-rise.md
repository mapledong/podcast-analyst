## Key Facts

> **F1** Rise: 44, already past the odds. Extreme poverty, log cabin, rural Ohio, fatherless before his second birthday. Janitor money for the first year of college. Professor of ancient languages, literature, and mathematics. An original proof published a month before the tape's scene — the journal astonished a member of Congress had written it. Education was salvation; it freed him from grinding poverty. 20th president. Only 199 days before the assassination. Millard is the reason Senra picked the book.

> **F2** War and the un-campaign: Middle Creek — attack from a second front, then a third; Marshall retreats; Kentucky stays Union; promotion to brigadier. He walks into a clearing of 'sleeping' soldiers and something goes out of him that never comes back: the sacredness of life. Still an ardent abolitionist: by thundering volley must this rebellion be met. Ten months after Middle Creek, elected to Congress; he will not sit until Lincoln asks, more than a year on. Lincoln dares not risk a single House vote and needs men who know the army. He will not fight for his own political future. Presidential fever ruins friends. He looks the other way — and is called anyway.

> **F3** Death as the lesson: 199 days. Guiteau and the medical circus (Millard's madness-and-medicine subtitle) kill him more surely than the bullet. Senra ties it to founders who are drafted: the best leaders are compelled. UNH and JNJ are the modern medicine-quality rhyme, not period stocks. BRK.B is the Senra habit of pointing at character compounders. The tape is a warning against seeking the chair and against 1881-quality systems around a wounded principal.

## Mental Model · *Education as Salvation × Drafted Not Seeking × Medicine Kills*

> **Components**
>
> Janitor to proof to brigadier to a chair he would not seek. War teaches that life is not sacred and he stays in anyway. Lincoln's one vote is enough to pull him off the field. 199 days. The assassin is half the story; the doctors are the rest. Founders who wait to be called still need a system that will not kill them. Keep the method boring enough to repeat every week. Numbers in the room beat stories in the hallway. The founder who will not walk the floor, make the tenth call, or rewrite the Saturday software is already the cartel.
>
> **Application**
>
> UNH and JNJ are the medicine-quality names — Millard's point is that bad care finishes the wound. BRK.B is character-over-campaign. Fade anyone campaigning for the chair as Garfield's negative. Size 'drafted CEO' stories as this tape, and then check the doctors.
