## Key Facts

> **F1** Bio arc: formal methods/PL research (IJCAR best-paper adjacent work) → see LLM systems shift inside Google-era PaLM/LaMDA before ChatGPT → LMSYS nights-and-weekends → SGLang → Databricks attempt to host development → Oct 2024 join xAI to co-lead Grok inference and force SGLang into frontier practice → spin out RadixArk with $100M seed from near-full set of Valley compute strategics.

> **F2** Philosophy: model-first companies compromise infra aesthetics; Sheng wants infra itself as product and romance. ADHD framed as trait/manual, not disease; breakthrough proofs while watching Zhen Huan Zhuan. Intensity mandatory. Openness: SGLang belongs to LMSYS community; RadixArk contributions stay open, no private fork; inference serving is a starting point not the long core — hence friendly posture to Baseten ($300M raise / $5B narrative cited) and Fireworks.

> **F3** Org lesson: mid-2024 volunteer bandwidth collapsed — couldn't deliver partner asks while moonlighting; anxiety that the 'hollow center' behind SGLang growth needed a company. Long mission: open infra so frontier capability isn't only for mega-labs; LMSYS remains the lifelong institution if/when the company ends; coexistence with strong AI requires human equality/solidarity first.

## Key Insights

> **1.** Infra is the product.
>
> **Q** Why not just serve model labs?
>
> **A** Model-first orgs compromise systems beauty; Sheng wants the stack itself to be the craft and the company.

> **2.** Open source hit a delivery wall.
>
> **Q** Why incorporate?
>
> **A** Night/weekend LMSYS couldn't meet exploding partner demand — hollow center had to be filled with full-time force.

> **3.** Don't compete with your users on hosting.
>
> **Q** RadixArk vs Baseten?
>
> **A** Engine stays open; long core isn't renting GPUs — coexistence over zero-sum inference SaaS.
