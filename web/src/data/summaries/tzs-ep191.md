## Key Facts

> **F1** Metalens (metasurface optics): collapses lens function into ultra-thin structures that can couple to CIS; industry narrative includes a Xiaomi phone front-camera CIS example, while Apple and peers have not fully adopted — route and yield still unfinished. Thinner form factor and optical degrees of freedom vs traditional lenses; materials/process and system integration constrain scale.

> **F2** Glass substrate and hybrid bonding: the market's 'glass substrate' usually means glass for FCBGA-class substrates — better CTE/stiffness than organic BT/core for advanced packaging. Volume 'origin' ≈2026; real product penetration may slip to 2027+. Treat claims that a whole GPU generation is already on glass with skepticism. Hybrid bonding is a core advanced-packaging interconnect path tied to TSMC-ecosystem roadmaps.

> **F3** SiC: clearest commercialization path of the four — power devices meet EV/industrial demand; substrate inch climb (6″→8″→12″, PVT growth, etc.) is the industry story. Many domestic makers still on 6″; finance often underweights substrate yield and ramp tempo. Examples are not advice; tech names are volatile.
