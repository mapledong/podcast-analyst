## Key Facts

> **F1** Cosmos 3 is NVIDIA's open world model: they open-source the model, the training framework, and part of the synthetic data, and say a better downstream model should also be open. Path ran Transfer (make sim video more real) → Reason → Cosmo-style pixel-change ≈ action-change. Personal mandate is Physical AI — chores, dangerous labor, dexterous hands he noticed more on this China trip — not competing with Sora-like consumer video.

> **F2** Org contrast: frontier labs have gone closed, papers hiding the key bits. Liu: NVIDIA was a design house that needed partners, so 'take care of others' is industrial structure, not a slogan. Jensen's one-line on him is GSD. OpenAI/Anthropic-class labs productized research; NVIDIA research must serve Thor, Isaac, and developer tools. He reads OpenAI's Sora de-emphasis as a coding-agent token-volume bet (24–48h agents vs a minute of chat) — defend everywhere and strength thins.

> **F3** Bio: NCTU Hsinchu wireless classmates → MediaTek/TSMC; he left for US PhD because protocol speedups felt incremental versus 0-to-1 US work. Early NVIDIA pitch was data-driven graphics (novel-in, movie-out) that traditional pipelines cannot staff. Kung fu since 18 under Xu Ji in the Li Shuwen / Cangzhou line — the same long-horizon craft metaphor as world models. 20-year quest: stay inside a massive infrastructure company and still ship open science.

## Mental Model · *Open World Model × Partner-Not-Opponent × Physical-AI Mandate*

> **Components**
>
> Publish weights, trainer, and some data so the Physical AI stack compounds. Refuse opponent framing because NVIDIA's original design-house P&L required customers to win. Put research behind robots/hands/sim, not a closed consumer video race. Accept that labs will pivot (Sora→coding) when token economics shift.
>
> **Application**
>
> NVDA is a Physical AI tools vendor (Thor/Isaac/Cosmos) as much as a GPU monopoly. Track whether Cosmos stays actually open when customers ship better models. TSMC/MediaTek remain the classmate industrial base. Fade 'NVIDIA must beat OpenAI at Sora' takes — Liu explicitly will not fight every front.

## Key Insights

> **1.** You don't need to defeat your opponents.
>
> **Q** Why open-source a world model inside NVIDIA?
>
> **A** A better model built on their work should also be open; partners, not opponents. Design-house history makes customer success the P&L.

> **2.** Physical AI is the assigned quest.
>
> **Q** Why not chase Sora?
>
> **A** Hands, chores, dangerous labor, Thor/Isaac. OpenAI's coding pivot shows even labs cannot defend everywhere.

> **3.** GSD is the internal review.
>
> **Q** What kind of researcher survives at NVIDIA?
>
> **A** An engineering leader who ships Cosmos inside a systems company — not a paper-only identity.
