## Key Facts

> **F1** Why the field exists: we know how to train and what comes out, not the middle. Aryaman (and the cold open) calls relying on people with strong intuition 'a bit insane' and attributes unexplained wins to divine will. Goal is to read the internal world model a next-token model must have — how people interact, why it is right, why it hallucinates, why it judges a civilian and a safety researcher differently (Transluce). Concept/behavior/'personality' directions show up as internal representations.

> **F2** J-space and hidden math: one reason for the interview is Anthropic's J-space paper — an internal workspace. Example: a web-weaving animal has eight legs, but 'spider' never appears in the chain. Jacobian / activation-space directions can point at words ≈10 tokens early. Circuits / Biology of LLMs: three-digit addition can hide intermediates while neurons track ones and tens. Golden Gate Claude locked a steering vector on the bridge. Aryaman guesses Anthropic is still the shop that would use classifiers or vectors in production (Fable-class research or bio-risk). Steer too hard and math dies; a finetune is cleaner.

> **F3** Control is the miss: Aryaman and Wu Zhengxuan built a steering benchmark — change outputs cleanly without hurting capability. Every interpretability method struggled. Best levers remain a prompt or a finetune. 'We do not have any technique that is actually good at controlling the model's internal thoughts.' If it is doing something bad, people retrain on better data; nobody edits the thought. Chain-of-thought helps (pre-CoT models were worse ≈1.5–2 years ago) but is not the same as reading unspoken steps. Alignment-by-interp is still a research program.

## Mental Model · *World Model Inside × Steering Demo ≠ Control × Personality as Representation*

> **Components**
>
> A next-token model that works must contain a world model — interp is reading it. Circuits prove hidden arithmetic. Golden Gate is a fun vector, not a production control plane; prompts and finetunes still win the steering bench. Transluce's user-conditioned behavior is the safety tell. Personalities are directions, not marketing. Do not wait for a thought-editor; we do not have one.
>
> **Application**
>
> Anthropic is the named lab (circuits, Golden Gate, possible production steering). Transluce is the user-conditioning print. OpenAI/others stay in the black-box default. Do not underwrite 'we can steer away bio-risk' as a product claim. Fade interp startups that sell control; the tape says they lose to a prompt.
