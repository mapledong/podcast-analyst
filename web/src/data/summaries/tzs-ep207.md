## Key Facts

> **F1** The book is a thinking tool with a tiny print. He finished it around September 2024. After the September 24, 2024 policy turn, stock books got crowded and this one slipped a year or two. About 3,000 copies, cover near 40 yuan, author share 15–20%, so roughly 10,000 yuan. A paid community at 1,000 yuan a head can clear 100,000 yuan faster. He wrote it anyway, by hand in early 2024 before chat tools were good, because a book forces a system: financials, valuation, business model. He later sent the file to ChatGPT. The model said the method is fine and the hole is portfolio construction and when to buy or sell — the eye stays on the single name and misses the book. That is the supplement he wants this hour to add.

> **F2** Agency, then two case files. Own money can wait three flat years. Other people's money gets two months. From April to June they ask why he did not own more AI. In August they ask why he did not sell. He bought AI-up-50% as the benchmark and Moutai down 10 points as the holding that makes him look stupid. Zijin, in his Deng Xiaofeng review: bought around 2019, stock up 10 times since, profit up about 4 times. About 60–70% of the 10 times is the commodity price. About 40% is profit. Strip the price and a bad six-year hold is still about 2 to 2.5 times from profit growth. Apple is the multiple file: bought when growth was soft and the balance sheet was net cash, earnings he puts near $50 billion a year, the multiple from the teens to the thirties. Price, profit, and multiple are three different checks. A 60,000-yuan unit profit that becomes 8,000 under auction pricing breaks the model; a cost-plus price that steps up is the comfortable path.

> **F3** Two circuits. US listed names fell from about 7,000 to about 5,000. After the 1990s the bar rose: a few hundred delistings a year, fewer IPOs than exits, a market that feels like it is there to make money. China, in his telling, lists companies to fund a technology shift, so supply keeps coming and the index experience is worse. US market cap sits in the first two or three hundred names. A-shares will not allow that concentration, and the small-cap tail matters. S&P 100 ROE and ROIC rose from 2011 to 2023. China's went down. A Goldman note he cites puts local state holdco ROIC under 1. US mix is consumption, health, and tech, plus buybacks. Chinese listed companies hoard cash the way households do. Leverage: Chinese households and firms borrow. The US borrower is the federal government. Pensions there are a standing bid for equities. Here, equities are small next to banks, retail is a large share of both assets and turnover, there is no real short, and the sales loop is product push. There, stocks are the core asset, institutions hold them, and the client pays the manager.

## Mental Model · *Single-Name Method × Price versus Profit × Two Circuits*

> **Components**
>
> The book is the single-name method. ChatGPT's hole is the portfolio and the trade. Own capital can wait. Outside capital benchmarks you against AI up 50 and Moutai down 10. Split a 10-times Zijin into price (about 60–70%) and profit (about 4 times, or 2–2.5 times with the price stripped). Apple is the multiple moving from the teens to the thirties on about $50 billion of earnings. The US circuit delists, concentrates, raises ROE, and has pensions as a bid. The China circuit lists to fund a shift, hoards cash, borrows at the household and the firm, and sells funds to retail. A 3,000-copy book at 40 yuan is the system. It is not the P&L.
>
> **Application**
>
> 2899.HK is the 2019-to-now file: 10 times, with most of it the metal price and a still-positive profit residual. AAPL is the multiple expansion he walks, from a soft growth year and net cash to the thirties. 600519.SZ is the agency pain, down 10 points while AI ran, not a broken-product short. Do not buy the index because the US delists more. Underwrite which of the three checks — price, profit, multiple — you are being paid.
