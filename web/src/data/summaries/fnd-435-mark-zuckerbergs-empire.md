## Key Facts

> **F1** House and scale: Edward and Karen look you in the eye. Dad is a ham; mom is shy. Dad would have guessed middle daughter Donna as the smart one. Four bright kids; Mark was in the right place at the right time. Mom: true interest shows in childhood. Empire language from the Aeneid — empire without end. At Harvard the republic-to-empire frame is already in the piece. Today: 3.6 billion users, $1.6 trillion market cap, majority voting control that makes removal effectively impossible. In 50 years he could still be ruler. 300,000 years of biology do not give a 19-year-old the keys to that room.

> **F2** Dictator's dilemma: the more power, the more he depends on others for news of his own court; fear and awe make them mute. Heads of state squeeze him over elections and worse, then leave office and die. He is still there. By 30 he networked billions; by 32 the advertising machine; by 42 one in 2.3 living people — more humans than existed in 1965. Annual revenue well above $200 billion. This year's capex exceeds every military budget except the US, China, and Russia. Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp each have more MAUs than living Christians.

> **F3** Stay and spend: peers abdicate, sell, get overthrown, or appoint a regent. Not him. 200 years of peace will not come for free — wake up, hit people, pay them to hit you, another subpoena. The tape says he is behind on AI and is answering with foundational-model spend and the Facebook-to-Meta rename after the Reality Labs years. Senra's founder rhyme is childhood passion plus refusal to leave the field.
