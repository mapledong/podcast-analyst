## Key Facts

> **F1** Print: Moderna + Merck INTerpath-001 Phase 3 — personalized mRNA + Keytruda after melanoma resection, better recurrence-free survival than PD-1 alone. Tape (and cold open) says the stock ripped more than 200% intraday, rare for biotech. Ying: that confirms Moderna is not a capped infectious-disease name; add oncology and the TAM is 'stars and sea,' and therapeutic pricing is not prophylactic pricing. Founded 2010 on an mRNA platform. Whatever the next readout, they opened a road for the class.

> **F2** How the product works: resect, sequence tumor-specific mutations, some become neoantigens (proteins the body has never seen), rank them, write a patient-specific mRNA, LNP-deliver, train T cells on residual disease. Keytruda releases the PD-1 brake; the vaccine names the enemy. Ying's stack in 5–10 years: scalpel + personalized vaccine + PD-1, and every approved PD-1 indication should sequentially get an mRNA vaccine. Cancer vaccines are decades old; Phase 3 is the new door.

> **F3** CMC is the limit: personalized therapy is the edge of process and of regulation. Industry wants drug in 4–6 weeks so treatment starts in the normal recovery window; miss it and the tumor grows back. A 1,000-patient trial is not 1,000 units on one day — commercial scale is one machine per patient, on-time, no cross-contamination. Ying does not fear the unknown after Boston; biotech is supposed to be turbulent. AI's job on this tape is antigen ranking, not replacing the plant. Moderna, BioNTech, and Chinese names (Abogen) are on different technical bets.
