## Key Facts

> **F1** Path and filter: undergrad business, master's management science. 2014-era trading was news, charts, Chan lun, trend books. 2016 first job was a domestic PE doing listed and half-primary (private placements / negotiated transfers), hoping to be a fifth-to-tenth shareholder and push operations or capital allocation. Pipeline math: chat 200–300 listed names a year, maybe 100 management talks, then ≈50, then three deals — one check 30 million RMB or two-to-three hundred million. A parallel track started when his boss, who had made a pile in 2014–15, sent him to audit a Beida value-investing course taught by Chang Jin (whisper: 常静), partner of Li Lu (whisper: 李鹿). Autumn sessions 2016–2020. Only after 2023 did he cut: high-quality-company value investing, not every PE process.

> **F2** Cycles and the missed compounder: first research name was Pangda Group, then the largest A-share auto 4S dealer, now delisted; Hong Kong's Zhongsheng (美东汽车) is the surviving peer and was smaller then. A placement name from that era also delisted. A colleague studied Moutai near 400 RMB in 2018; 2018–2021 it ran to about 2700–2800. Yongqing was a bystander: already from 80–100 to 400–500, so 'it must be done.' Even held to now the CAGR is still fine. 2024 first half was ugly; after September the tape reversed. An August 2024 entry sat one month; a 2021 entry sat three years. China cycles are not US cycles. 200x PE: wait. 15x PE plus five points of growth: then decide.

> **F3** Why this school: only a few companies cross time and make people real money; most research expires next year. Cognitive compounding lives in top consumer, internet, and resources (copper, gold, silver, oil) that stay. Personality: he does not want a contact list of names he dislikes — walk with 1% of companies and partners. Money that cannot sit two to three years should not use a ten-year strategy (a recent 'this shop listed and the fund looks smart' print is luck plus duration). High-quality names are more transparent; bosses like Ma Huateng still take the earnings call. Masters already wrote the internet / consumer / cycle books — cross-check them. Duan Yongping (whisper: 段亚平) told Buffett Apple beat Coca-Cola; Buffett meets a hundred such people a year. You are not Buffett or Munger in a room. Circle of competence is tiny: calling Moutai a good company does not tell you to buy a 20% or 50% drawdown.

## Mental Model · *1% Names × Money Duration × Circle Is Not Good-Company*

> **Components**
>
> Filter like the PE year: 300 chats to three checks. Take the Beida/Li Lu class as a second job until you can cut. High-quality value is a 2023 choice, not a 2014 slogan. Research only compounds on trades that persist. Match strategy to money that can sit two to three years. Read cycles as pattern recognition (2024 H1 vs September; 2021 vs August entries), not as a forecast. Ability circle is high-conviction judgment, not a quality label. Open collaborative research plus a small partner set is how ideas reach you when you are not Buffett.
>
> **Application**
>
> 600519.SZ is the missed 400-to-2700 print and the 'good company ≠ buy the dip' test, not a 2026 entry. TCEHY is the self-requiring-boss print (Ma still does the call). AAPL is Duan's example to Buffett, not a new size. Fade ten-year venture stories funded with one-year money. Do not copy a 200x name; wait for something closer to 15x. Pangda/Zhongsheng is the 4S graveyard: biggest is not safest.

## Key Insights

> **1.** Method before ticker.
>
> **Q** Why not just pick a school and a stock?
>
> **A** Seven lose, two flat, one wins. 2014 charts and 300-to-3 PE work both failed to be the job. The 2023 cut is high-quality names that last.

> **2.** Moutai was a bystander tape.
>
> **Q** What did 400 to 2700 teach?
>
> **A** A good company already up 4–5x can still compound. Calling it done at 400 was the error. Circle of competence is not the quality label.

> **3.** Money has a duration.
>
> **Q** When is value the wrong tool?
>
> **A** If the cash cannot sit two to three years, do not run a ten-year strategy. 2024 H1 vs a one-month August sit is the China-cycle print.
