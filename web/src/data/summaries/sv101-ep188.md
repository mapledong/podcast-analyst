## Key Insights

> **1.** Schools simulate the next civilization stage.
>
> **Q** Does the factory model still work?
>
> **A** Post-industrial schools copied factory hours and division of labor; the AI era should simulate projects, creation, and end-to-end value flow. University research enclaves (Princeton IAS-like) stay irreplaceable, but much certification sinks to K-12 portfolios within 5–10 years.

> **2.** Perceiving love is a trainable core competency.
>
> **Q** What anchors internal abundance?
>
> **A** Happiness should be practiced like a skill; perceiving love looks inward (birdsong, trees, daily gifts), not outward grasping. Wang lost this in 2021–2022 debt crisis; recovery aligned mission and parenting — soul engineering maintains unity-with-all-things.

> **3.** Hand-craft the chip before the factory.
>
> **Q** Biggest ten-year lesson?
>
> **A** Education accrues trust cohort-by-cohort like media or semiconductors — not internet viral replication. Early oversized mission without self-cultivation; big campus + training license + pandemic + double reduction chain reaction. Rejected mergers knowing cycle volatility sacrifices mission; 500 students is the responsible ceiling.
