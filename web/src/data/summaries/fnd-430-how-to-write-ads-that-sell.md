## Key Facts

> **F1** Two books sold together: Scientific Advertising (earlier) and My Life in Advertising. Preface: this is a business story for successors, not a memoir; 'any man who learns more about anything than others owes a statement.' Hopkins says he lived in a vortex of advertising and worked twice as long as anybody else in the field.

> **F2** Schlitz case: hired when the beer was fifth in share. First step is research, not slogans. Brewery tour — plate-glass rooms with beer dripping over pipes in filtered air, 4,000-foot artisan wells, six-month vats, lab mother yeast developed through 1,200 experiments. The facts were industry-standard; the ad was being first to say them specifically.

> **F3** Senra's use-case: his interview show lives in 3–6 minute clips; he is rereading Hopkins to distill one line / one headline. Ogilvy (garbled as 'Olgrevy' in captions) built an agency on these books. Recurring Hopkins ideas: test, record research, blaze the trail, specifics over adjectives.

## Mental Model · *Research First × Specifics Everyone Has × Tested Copy*

> **Components**
>
> Walk the factory before you write a line. Hopkins's Schlitz tour produced filtered-air cooling rooms, 4,000-foot artisan wells, six-month aging vats, and a mother yeast from 1,200 experiments — facts every brewer already had. Being first to say them specifically was the ad. Record the research: pioneers owe a written trail; Lasker locked Scientific Advertising in a safe for 20 years because it gave away the firm's secrets. Ogilvy later treated both books as the foundation of his agency. Senra is stealing the same compression for 3–6 minute founder clips: one headline, then the proof. Hopkins claims he worked twice as long as anyone in the field.
>
> **Application**
>
> Any consumer brand still hiding process facts (purity, wells, aging, lab counts) has a Hopkins ad sitting in the plant. Do not start with a slogan. Founder media: distill to one sentence. Treat 'we do what everyone does, we just say it' as a real campaign, not a cynic's joke. The autobiography is the obligation to successors, not a memoir.
