## Key Facts

> **F1** Framing: Birkin scarcity culture vs family absurdity — 6M Hermès shares (~€10B; '200+ high-speed train cars of Birkins') disappear from fifth-generation heir Nicholas Puech. He sues ex-advisor Freymond and LVMH/Arnault (May prior year), suspecting a setup despite little operational overlap between houses.

> **F2** Personal thread: pandemic-era gardener household gifted 50+ properties; gardener's wife catches Freymond lying about a CHF1M transfer → audit → missing shares. Freymond accused of aggravated breach of trust; accounts/art frozen; dies by train near Swiss chalet two weeks after questioning (ruled suicide). Probe: 2001 LVMH Geneva contact; 2008 ≈4.8M shares into Dilico then SocGen swap leg at ~€86.66 vs >€1600 now.

> **F3** Macro war: Arnault builds stake via three banks — Natixis ≈5.0M (4.7%), SocGen ≈4.8M (4.5%), Amundi ≈3.2M (3.0%) — each under banner thresholds; switches cash settlement to physical after June 2010 when legendary CEO Dumas is terminally ill. Oct 23, 2010 disclosure shock. Coda: Apr 15 print Q1 sales €4.07B (−1.4%) and −14% intraday crash — largest in Hermès history cited.
