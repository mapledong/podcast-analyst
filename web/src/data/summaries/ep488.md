## Background

> Patrick wants a supply-side tour from transistor to token. Movva's first job was NVIDIA kernels; he hires for love of performance engineering, not years of CUDA. Theme of the company is abundance — treat intelligence as a commodity, not an expensive consultant.

---

## Key Facts

> **F1** Product: Sal Research is a token factory API for open (and customer-sovereign) models. Thesis: whenever something is 10× cheaper it is a new category. He will squeeze more tokens from the same NVIDIA GPU than anyone else, starting at kernels, then every chip/power/land lever in the US. Contrast: Baseten, Fireworks, Together chased low-latency because of Cursor; he thinks that was right a year ago and is now the wrong scarcity.

> **F2** Workload mix: humans cannot consume 10× more interactive tokens; the slack is background/proactive agents. He forecasts ≈50/50 background vs realtime by year-end, then 9010 in favor of background. Long-context (200k) is not the model's core strength if pretrain was 8–16k. Cybersecurity batch (Fable/Mythos-style sweep of every line of code) is the kind of job that wants cheap tokens, not 100ms chat.

> **F3** Hardware: happy to buy a good fraction of any new chip if TSMC wafers exist — Etched, d-Matrix, etc. fail first on allocation, not ideas. Cerebras: on-die SRAM vs HBM (≈10 TB/s on Blackwell-class vs much higher on wafer-scale, different capacity). He will not pretend CUDA years are a hiring filter. Customers increasingly want to own/sovereign the intelligence they depend on.

## Mental Model · *Token Factory × Background Mix × Wafer Gate*

> **Components**
>
> Compete on dollars per token and throughput, not Cursor-class latency. Humans cannot consume 10x more interactive tokens; slack is background and proactive agents. Movva ends 2026 around 50/50 realtime vs background and wants 9010 background. Fireworks, Together, and Baseten optimized for last year's Cursor pull. New ASICs (Etched, d-Matrix) die first on TSMC wafer allocation, not ideas. Cerebras SRAM vs HBM is a capacity/bandwidth trade, not a religion. Hire for performance obsession, not CUDA years. Customers want sovereign/open weights they can own.
>
> **Application**
>
> Do not assume latency vendors win the next inference dollar. NVDA remains the default substrate he still squeezes first. Size ASIC startups on wafers they can actually get. Background-token jobs (whole-codebase security sweeps, hour-to-week sandboxes) are the 2026–27 tell. Treat Sal Research as a cheap-token factory, not another chat API. Long-context 200k is not magic if pretrain was 8–16k.

## Key Insights

> **1.** 10× cheaper tokens is a new product.
>
> **Q** Why not just be another inference API?
>
> **A** Interactive humans are saturated. Background agents and whole-codebase sweeps need a factory, not a chat box.

> **2.** Latency vendors optimized for Cursor.
>
> **Q** Is that still the scarce job?
>
> **A** Movva says that was last year's pull. He wants cheapest tokens and 9010 background.

> **3.** ASICs die at TSMC, not in the deck.
>
> **Q** What does he diligence first?
>
> **A** Wafer allocation. He will buy a slice of any chip that can actually ship.
