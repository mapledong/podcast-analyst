## Key Facts

> **F1** Cash vs build: five hyperscalers' combined buybacks $48B (2021 Q4 peak) → $12.9B last year Q4 → $4.6B 2026 Q1, down ≈90%. Only Microsoft still buying; Google stopped in Q1; Meta three straight zero quarters; Oracle < $100M last FY; Amazon none since mid-2022. Epoch (June): operating cash +≈23%/yr vs capex +≈70%, lines cross 2026 Q3 — 'collectively broke' before 2027 if the slope holds. Morgan Stanley: $2.9T global DC capex through 2028, $1.4T from hyperscaler cash, $1.5T external. Bond issuance $28B/yr 2020–24 → $108.3B in 2025 → almost $200B YTD through July 2026 (≈6× the old annual). Microsoft issued no public debt 2024–now. Filings avoid the words data center / AI — Meta's $30B deal and Oracle's 2026-02 print say general corporate purposes (Oracle even said dividends and buybacks the next day).

> **F2** Why not just sell more public bonds: (1) oversubscription thinned — Amazon 5.3× (Nov) to 1.6× (2026-07) as hundreds of billions stacked in one window; (2) new-issue concession 2.25 bp median 2025 → 12 bp 2026, Amazon's long tranche +18–21 bp; (3) the 'cash machine, no debt, buybacks' compact with quality funds breaks; (4) on-balance-sheet debt hits ratings. Oracle capex $21.2B → $55.7B in a year; S&P BBB → BBB- in July, one notch from junk, which can lose insurers/pensions. They want more money, longer tenor (DC payback 10–20 years), and off-balance-sheet optics — public IG cannot deliver all three.

> **F3** Private credit fills the hole after SaaS souring: Blue Owl fund saw 40% redemption two straight quarters and paid <15%; stock $24 → $9. BIS (July): 60% of PC loans to SaaS borrowers already borrowed from 7+ lenders vs <10% a decade ago. Software stocks ~−40% from 2025-09 highs even as M365 growth 15%→19%. DC securities $1.3B (2022) → $30–40B, ≈20×. Case: Meta Hyperion — $27.3B at Beignet Investor / Laidley LLC in Louisiana, Blue Owl OSNL on top, Delaware shells in <6 weeks, no SEC prospectus. Blue Owl 63% of Beignet Investor and 80% of the PropCo; Meta's 20% is the $2.37B on its books, so $27.29B at 6.581% to 2049-05 stays off-balance-sheet. Pelican Leap lease from 2029, 4-year initial term, residual guarantee ≈$28B. Same-tenor Meta unsecured ≈5.5% — ≈$300M extra interest/year for the clean sheet. July Sopaipilla (El Paso) $12.5B at 7.534% to 2048, BlackRock in the 80% seat; two Meta DC deals ≈$40B in under a year.

## Mental Model · *Shadow Borrowing × Rating Cliff × Residual Guarantee*

> **Components**
>
> Hyperscalers need more cash, longer tenor, and a clean consolidated sheet. Public IG is slower, dearer, and fully on-balance-sheet — so SPV + lease + residual guarantee moves $27B off Meta while Meta still pays. Price of cleanliness is ≈100 bp (5.5% vs 6.581%) and a one-notch haircut (AA- name, A+ paper). Oracle is the on-balance-sheet warning: capex jump plus BBB-. Blue Owl is recycling SaaS credit risk into DC paper after a redemption freeze. Buyback collapse is the real-economy tell that FCF is staying inside the building.
>
> **Application**
>
> ORCL is the stressed public print (rating, capex, prospectus mismatch). META's Hyperion/Sopaipilla stack is hidden leverage — add lease + residual to the model even if GAAP is clean. MSFT's no-issue / still-buyback tape is the quality exception. AMZN concession and 1.6× books show the public window is tiring. Do not treat $1.65T as 'disappeared'; it is Meta-backed and Blue Owl / BlackRock-held. A SaaS-loan maturity wall (11% by 2027, 20% by 2028) is why this paper exists.
