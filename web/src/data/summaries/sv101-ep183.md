## Key Insights

> **1.** Top capital still underwrites people first.
>
> **Q** Why $4.65B with no product?
>
> **A** Roadmaps expire; investors buy teams that can re-aim weekly and already earned trust at Meta/DeepMind/Salesforce exits.

> **2.** Auto-research is the product.
>
> **Q** What ships?
>
> **A** A machinery that produces impressive research outcomes while methods stay proprietary — research-as-product.
