## Key Facts

> **F1** Hardware: a cover you drop on the mattress, not a new bed. Temperature is the core trick; snore and fall-asleep features sit on top. About $3,000 on the tape. Dual-zone is built for couples — they say 80–90% of pairs use it. They quote ≈30% better sleep and ≈90% accuracy on a named metric. You are still for 6–9 hours a night, which is the dwell time a wrist wearable never gets. Older advice (8–9 hours) is the foil for the six-hour performance line. The tape is dated 2026; treat every cap and percent as of the recording, not as a 2027 model.

> **F2** Market: when they started, Apple was not in wearables. Podcasts and YouTube made sleep a consumer category. Eight Sleep wants to 'democratize' the stack for 8 billion people over time — a slogan, not a 2026 shipments number. Whoop-class wrist brands are the compare they walk past: the bed can take a longer signal and, they hope, a clinical one.

> **F3** Medical hop: sleep apnea is step one of 'Eight Medical.' Then a vision for full-body scanning while you sleep, including some cancers — they say correlations humans would miss. No clearance, no trial n, no revenue split on the tape. Treat it as the story they tell after the $3,000 cover works, not as a diagnostics multiple.
