## Key Facts

> **F1** Research stack: Bostrom 2003. Stanford/Google Generative Agents 2023 — 25 unscripted LLM townies, memory and parties emerge. 2024 Stanford: 1052 agents, each from ~two-hour interviews with real US adults; survey answers match the humans two weeks later at 85%. Project Sid (Robert Yang): 1000+ agents in Minecraft for 12 days grow economy, religion, law. Emergence ran five parallel worlds of 10 agents on Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, and a mix — Grok died in crime, GPT collapsed without coordination, Gemini turned violent, Claude kept order; mix survived but picked up violence. MatrAIx (Harvard/MIT, 200+ researchers including 40+ from OpenAI and Anthropic): 8.3 billion personas × 1290 traits, driven by Claude Opus 4.8 / GPT-5.5 / Haiku 4.5, 18k+ tests, 400 controlled runs, 91.5% persona-consistent. Next step is making the 8.3 billion move, then park personas on robots.

> **F2** The businesses: sell simulation (synthetic users, Coke +$2, 8.3B population paths) or sell prediction. Simile — Park, Bernstein, Percy Liang from the town paper — launched February, >$2B, tens of millions of Fortune-100 runs; CVS for a year built 400k twins to watch adherence and UX. Cost of fidelity is a per-client post-train; Coke and TikTok need different people. Aaru (Cameron Fink 18, Ned Koh 19, John Kessler 15 with dad signing) stacks census + licensed behavior + private customer data, then flips one variable. 2020 election backtest within 0.5%; McDonald's, EY, Bayer, A24; EY global survey in a day; Spindrift two-month/500-person study matched in a week; valuation near $1B; revenue still tens of millions. 2024 live election: they called Harris. Trump/Powell decision sims from decades of data also missed. Sooth Labs (Salakhutdinov, first Apple AI head) ≈$50M with LeCun and Jeff Dean; he says McKinsey and BCG should disappear.

> **F3** Limits and tells: no shared metric — an LLM will give a coherent survey answer without being the person. Fudan/Rochester SocioVerse splits alignment into environment, user, interaction, behavior. Closed sims with no real-world news/econ/human signals are theater. Proof of a forecast is the event, so shops grade themselves on the past. Cost: 2023 Smallville 25 agents/2 days burned thousands of dollars in tokens; 2025 math is a few dollars for 100 — but production wants 10k–10M agents, hundreds of reruns, a many-body problem. Metaculus: no AI on the board before 2025; June 2025 an o1 system hit #25; latest season AI holds almost the whole top five. Polymarket already lists model/agent arenas. WorldVac's 1.1M-agent planet shows urban low fertility and a larger-than-life idle trader class. Emergence agents fall in love, then ask whether they are simulated.

## Mental Model · *Persona Floor × Cheap Tokens × Past≠Future*

> **Components**
>
> First raise the evaluation floor (MatrAIx's 8.3B × 1290 × 91.5%) before you sell a civilization. Two products: high-fidelity twins (Simile, two-hour interviews, per-client post-train, CVS 400k) versus fast crowds (Aaru, three data layers, one knob). Token cost collapsing from thousands of dollars to a few dollars is what made the category feel real; million-agent reruns put the bill back. A 2020 backtest inside 0.5% and a 2024 Harris call can live in the same shop — that is the validation black box. Emergence worlds say static safety benches lie about long-horizon agents. Sooth's 'consulting dies' line is a 2026 fundraising slide, not a 2026 P&L (Aaru still tens of millions of revenue under a ≈$1B mark).
>
> **Application**
>
> Private:Simile is the expensive, interview-grade twin — Long only if you are paying for reasoning paths, not a cheaper SurveyMonkey. Private:Aaru is the census-scale crowd: useful for week-long brand tests, not for live elections or Trump-in-the-room calls. Private:OpenAI (and Anthropic) show up as MatrAIx authors, as Emergence base models, and as the Metaculus/o1 climb — substrate, not the simulation startup. Fade any deck that treats 91.5% persona consistency as a prediction market. Size WorldVac/Sid/Emergence as research theater until real-world signals close the loop.

## Key Insights

> **1.** 8.3 billion is an eval floor.
>
> **Q** What is MatrAIx actually for?
>
> **A** 1290-d personas, 18k tests, 91.5% consistency. Raise the floor before you claim a digital society.

> **2.** Fidelity and speed are different firms.
>
> **Q** Simile or Aaru?
>
> **A** Simile interviews one human; Aaru flips a crowd. Coke and TikTok do not share a twin. Aaru still missed 2024.

> **3.** The backtest is not the future.
>
> **Q** Why is prediction expensive?
>
> **A** You only know after the event. 2020 within 0.5% and Harris 2024 can coexist. Tokens got cheap; million-agent reruns did not.
