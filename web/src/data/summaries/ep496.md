## Background

Win was execution plus integrations. Hour is about the SaaS-pocalypse multiple, token pricing, agent product, talent wars versus lab/tractor checks, the 101 billboard, and whether Vanta is a $50 billion or $250 billion company.

## Key Facts

> **F1** Category win and the multiple. Started ≈8 years ago; today ≈16,000 customers worldwide and about five products. Integrations into one of 400 tools feed a security data lake they call the trust graph — broader, she says, than other compliance vendors and some security vendors. Patrick's frame: still a very fast, very high-margin SaaS book that four years ago was 'the best business model ever,' now marked at a third or a quarter of the old multiple. She holds two pictures: an 80% growth / 80% margin company can return more cash over a long run than a 160% growth / 30% margin token seller, yet the latter can clear 3x, 4x, even 10x the multiple. Last 12 months felt like the SaaS-pocalypse: 'good job building a system of record, you're now screwed.' Bending Spoons is the grim-reaper joke in the hallway.

> **F2** Agent, tokens, pricing. The product is no longer a red-alert dashboard. An agent writes custom off-boarding when someone leaves, asks 'this once / always,' and keeps the user in flow — auto-generating UI when chat is the wrong surface. About 25% of customers are in that loop; the goal is 75% for retention, with commercialization later. Token pricing loses: mark up a Claude token 4x and the buyer asks if you are 4x Claude and goes to try the model. They eat the token bill and take lower margin. Decision-action work uses fewer tokens than coding agents, so they optimize for quality and swap models on task-specific evals written with ex-CISO experts. Outcome/output pricing is what everyone wants (ticket closed, account off-boarded); the overhead of 50 units is why they have not shipped it. Credits that hide a token FX rate get seen through.

> **F3** Talent, security, billboard. Top-person total comp is ≈10x five years ago and she thinks dispersion should be wider; bands are a joke versus market. A competing pitch: '$6 billion today, Sequoia writes $10 billion next month, someone marks it $25 billion — get in at 6.' She shows candidates the real ARR tape. Tenders are expected even if they feel unhealthy; 99% of big independent US software companies are public, so IPO is a means. CrowdStrike will do enterprise white-hat/patch better; Vanta's lane is customers who will not pay $150,000 for Wiz but will pay $40–50k, starting with AWS/Azure misconfig. French software firms switched to Chinese models when Trump closed Fable to non-Americans — continuity, not nationalism. Mid-2021 'compliance that doesn't suck too much' billboard near Burlingame went viral; they later lost the 101/280 spot after a crayon placement order, and a cash-flow-positive buyer refused $3 million to take a ≈$1.5 million contract. Next five years in one word: expansive.

## Mental Model · *Trust Graph × Eat-the-Tokens × ARR Over Vibes*

> **Components**
>
> Win a new category by shipping and by owning the integrations (400 tools) that make automation and agents possible. The SaaS-pocalypse marked the old 80/80 book at a third or a quarter; do not answer by selling marked-up tokens. Eat model cost, take lower margin, and get 25% of customers into an always-on agent loop on the way to 75%. Outcome pricing is the wish; token and credit schemes put you next to Claude. Hire and pay for high-agency people who already know Vanta and the customer — she would take a worse salesperson with company context. Post-training a model for the press is overhyped; FTE wrappers on five-figure contracts are Palantir cosplay. Security pressure rises because models chain 17 'low' issues instead of quitting after 9,000 tries.
>
> **Application**
>
> Private:Vanta is the name: 16,000 customers, five products, ARR as the scoreboard, $250 billion as recruiting talk. CRWD is the named enterprise pentest/patch winner she will not try to beat wall-to-wall; stay in the mid-market Wiz-gap ($40–50k vs $150k). PLTR is the eight-figure FTE analog she says does not map onto five- and six-figure deals. Fade token-priced compliance clones and 'we post-trained a model' decks. Size government and CISO budget that now clears because the ask is attached to AI. The 101 billboard is brand, not a moat — they already lost the spot.

## Key Insights

> **1.** 80/80 still compounds. The multiple does not.
>
> **Q** Did the business break, or the tape?
>
> **A** Nothing changed about fast growth and high margins. Token sellers can still clear 3–10x the multiple. She lives for the long run.

> **2.** Do not mark up Claude.
>
> **Q** Why eat token cost?
>
> **A** Buyers reverse the 4x and try the model. Get 25% → 75% onto the agent, then price.

> **3.** AI is high excitement, low trust.
>
> **Q** What is Vanta at $250B?
>
> **A** The connector between companies that must prove they are industrial-grade and the auditors, regulators, and customers who ask.
