## Key Facts

> **F1** Clock: Gangdisi 2019 after Laobai's fuzzy-algorithm youth and a dead job market. Three years of data, then June 2023 — months after OpenAI's consumer break — they ship their own AI research terminal. 2023: AI writes briefs. 2024–25: industry notes, stock work, investment memos for clients. 2026: self-training and what he calls a basically 100% result handed to the user. A K3-class model at 2.8 trillion parameters is the named upgrade. H200 on his cost tape: 1.5 million two years ago, 5.5 million now (treat as their marker, not a street print).

> **F2** FDE: Palantir trademarked the glow. Laobai says OpenAI and Anthropic job specs ignore coding and demand domain. His team has zero engineers — they drive FanBot-class agents and know what a 3G filing and a sector word mean. Taobao hired shoe people as xiao'er; he hires PMs. Product sprawl: ≈3,000 function points, about 30 in daily use; April talk of 40 skills versus a later 14–20-skill working set; 20 agents and 19 MCP hooks on one pass. More surface area is not more research.

> **F3** Style and the 20%: value, growth, and trend shops train the same agent differently — data diet, prompts, files. One client never lets AI pick a stock; he only watches upstream prints that move prices in days. Judgment — buy or pass — is the part AI cannot retire. User split on the tape: 20% want to live in it, 60% watch, 20% refuse. They staff the first 20. A >¥10 billion universe of ≈1,800 names is the named pond. When 'everyone' is at 80% capability, the edge moves. 24% gross-margin jumps and a 20-day couch holiday are color, not a model.

## Mental Model · *Domain FDE × Small Skill Surface × Human Buy Button*

> **Components**
>
> Hire finance people who can hear the client, then let an agent write the board. Keep 30 of 3,000 features. 2019 data plus a June 2023 terminal plus a 2026 self-train loop is the clock. K3 at 2.8T and a rising H200 bill are the infra prints. Style lives in the data diet, not in a bigger report pile. The 20/60/20 shop only pays for the first 20%. The buy button stays with the PM. The 20/60/20 split is why a 100% delivery line still sits on a niche attach.
>
> **Application**
>
> Gangdisi is a private research terminal — diligence the 20% attach, not the 100% delivery line. OpenAI is the 2023 trigger and the FDE job-spec compare. NVDA is the H200 cost marker. PLTR is the FDE brand they refuse to copy as a sales glow. Do not underwrite 'AI picks the stock.'
