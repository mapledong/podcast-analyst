## Key Facts

> **F1** DeepMind RT-1 lore: 13 robots collecting for 17 months in office/kitchen — still tiny vs LLM scrapes. Teleop is hard because operators lack robot proprioception/tactile feedback; mocap/YouTube miss robot-state. Mifeng pitches physical-AI data infra: ≈2M teleop hours capacity in 2026 plus ≈8M hours planned other paradigms.

> **F2** Gaps: Sim-to-Real (physics/contact mismatch), Embodiment Gap (human hand ≠ robot hand; occlusion corrupts state), Functional Retargeting (copying motion shape ≠ task intent). Tacmap translates fingertip deformation maps to help fine skills but inherits sim gaps. For complex tasks, trajectory ratios ~ teleop:mocap:web ≈ 1:100:10000 — quality over raw hours.

> **F3** Strategy split: Chinese OEMs industrialize teleop labor into factories as a moat; Valley faces expensive labor so pushes simulation and platformization. Goals differ — industrial 100% success vs consumer generalization with human handoff — so data mixes differ. Agibot claimed 10k embodied robots by late March; data key still open.
