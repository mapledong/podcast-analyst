## Key Facts

> **F1** Book and cluster: late 2021 Sanlian wanted a county-level manufacturing cover in the COVID structure debate. Huang picked Jinjiang/Chendai — the birthplace of every familiar name except Li Ning, who still put badminton R&D next door. The magazine piece became Xie Bang at Century Wenjing; he also wrote a lithium/EV cover book elsewhere. Method: ≈1,500 documents and a 360-degree pass. Path he draws: 1980s farmers making leather then athletic shoes to leave poverty; household division of labor; 1990s Taiwan transfer across the strait; Nike and Adidas cut-and-sew orders in 40-plus countries' supply maps; 1997–98 Asian crisis; 2000 brand-building; 2008 Olympic inventory wipe. That is the 40-year factory story, not a logo story. Forty-plus countries in the 1990s OEM map is why Jinjiang could take Nike/Adidas volume before it knew how to brand.

> **F2** Ding versus Li Ning: a hired Li Ning manager told Huang Anta meetings are loud and year-end is Ding leading 'love to fight is to win'; Li Ning meetings are slow, beautiful PPT. Ding walks stores — 400–500 market points in the crisis, several cities a day, WeChat steps 'at least 20,000' if you count that way — and makes sales people do the same. Huang: he took about four meetings a month and put ego down so FILA could be run by fashion people (Yao Weixiong out of L'Oréal/France). 2003 CBA after Kong Linghui; 2003 Shanghai Fengxian as an Adidas-Reebok agent to learn a premium counter; 2007 IPO; 2008 Olympic exam; 2009 China Olympic Committee and the FILA buy after watching Kappa (Chen Yihong, ex-Li Ning partner) double then die on wholesale. First FILA mistake: Anta clothes with a FILA mark. Fix: front-end independence, shared Jinjiang mid/back office, 24 seasons versus four-to-six order fairs, Italy archive trips, then DTC because Kappa's dealers had killed each other.

> **F3** Other Jinjiang and the winner test: most listed peers still do not give managers real weight. Qiaodan / Zhongqiao Sport (Ding Guoxiong) did after the inventory crisis — an Adidas Greater China / later Amer-Sports-class hire ran Feiying PB, the cheap carbon plate that sold as many Chinese runners' first plate at 80–90% of Nike function. Fastest payables in the cluster is the cost weapon. Huang's law: do not sit in the comfortable core. Anta's breakthroughs (FILA, later Descents on the tape) came when the main brand already felt fine. Li Ning is the milder, more manager-PPT house and the national-team brand that still could not hold fashion without a foreign plate. Nike/Adidas remain the OEM school and the ceiling. Super-Anta is Yongqing's earlier miss, not this book's chapter.

## Mental Model · *Jinjiang Cluster × Ego-Down FILA × Wholesale Trap*

> **Components**
>
> A county cluster that learned cut-and-sew from Taiwan and Nike/Adidas, then tried to brand after 2000 and almost died in 2008 inventory. Anta's edge is Ding walking 400–500 stores and hiring fashion people he will not overrule — four meetings a month, FILA on 24 waves and DTC after Kappa's dealer crash. Li Ning is the slower PPT shop. Qiaodan's Feiying PB is what happens when a manager owns a single hero SKU and payables stay short. 1,500 files and 360-degree work are how Huang built the 40-year clock. A foreign plate was the only on-tape way past the 'Chinese brand ceiling' Ding felt after CBA and the Olympic committee still did not move the needle. Count the 1,500 files as the clock, not a 2026 fashion week. Super-Anta is Yongqing's earlier miss; this tape is the 40-year factory diary. A Jinjiang name that still lives on wholesale fashion is Kappa waiting to happen again.
>
> **Application**
>
> 2020.HK is the FILA-plus-cluster compounder — diligence independence of the fashion P&L and store-walking, not the main Anta logo. 2331.HK is the milder national brand on this tape. NKE is the OEM school and the 80–90% function bar Feiying chased. Do not buy a Jinjiang name because 2008 is old news; wholesale fashion still kills. Walk the stores. Leave FILA alone.

## Key Insights

> **1.** Jinjiang is the factory; Li Ning is the exception.
>
> **Q** Where did the 40 years happen?
>
> **A** Chendai cluster: Anta, Xtep, 361°, Peak. Li Ning put badminton R&D next door. 1980 shoes → 1990s Nike/Adidas orders → 1997 crisis → 2000 brands → 2008 inventory.

> **2.** FILA worked when Ding left it alone.
>
> **Q** What did Kappa teach?
>
> **A** Fashion doubles, then wholesale kills it. Independent team, 24 waves, shared back office, DTC. Four meetings a month and 400–500 crisis store walks are the Ding print.

> **3.** A hero SKU needs a manager and short payables.
>
> **Q** What is Feiying PB?
>
> **A** Zhongqiao’s manager-led carbon plate at 80–90% of Nike function, cheaper. Fastest settlement in the cluster is the other half.
