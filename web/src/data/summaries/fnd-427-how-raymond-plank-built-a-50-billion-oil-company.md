## Key Facts

> **F1** Apache began with $250,000 in 1954; by mid-2008 that stake of capital had compounded toward a $50B enterprise narrative Plank frames as proof of human learning capacity into his 90s. Origin: maple-syrup/entrepreneurial Minnesota youth → tax & accounting service for small businesses → oil-investor clients reveal well economics after wartime under-drilling plus government tax incentives for domestic search.

> **F2** Insight: highly taxed individuals were incentivized into oil & gas; Plank expands services then into operating via Apache. Partner Truman Anderson conflict — 'values are not created equal' — short personal impatience vs Plank's long Apache horizon, complete with ashtray-smashing arguments. Acquisition rule: avoid era conglomerates' error of overlaying corporate overhead on founder-operators; buy, leave operators running, centralize only major capital calls.

> **F3** Operating edges: personal aircraft as deal-speed weapon ('get the plane'); persuasion via analogy without scripts; belief that markets rarely pause where you expect. Late-life learning claim — ninth decade faster learning — closes the memoir loop Senra loves: capacity exceeds early self-belief.
