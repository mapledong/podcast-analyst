## Key Facts

> **F1** The print: Goldman Sept 7, Bloomberg Ag spot +24% y/y, wheat the leader at +41%. Palm and rubber futures up with drought talk for SE Asian plantations. Zhang: Niño is not a buy-everything switch. It moves rain, heat, oceans, and water. Price only jumps if that hit lands on a crop's critical window, stocks are already low, exporters are concentrated, or policy/logistics tighten. A city rice-oil-flour metaphor: warehouses, cargoes on the water, and processor contracts usually absorb a regional miss. The tape is dated 2026; treat every cap and percent as of the recording, not as a 2027 model.

> **F2** Five-factor stack: (1) planted / grown / harvested / shipped; (2) feed, food, crush, ethanol, biodiesel, textiles; (3) stocks as the cushion; (4) export bans, tariffs, ports, canals, war freight; (5) cycle, dollar, rates, energy, fund positioning. Weather is the biggest supply wild card but has no fixed percent of price. Fat stocks + many exporters = shrug. Thin stocks + two countries = a 1–2% yield miss gets a fat risk premium.

> **F3** Fertilizer tangent: most of the world makes nitrogen from gas; China uses a more stable domestic route. Neighbors' prices still leak. A longer story — drought cuts hydro, gas goes to power, less gas for urea, plus Hormuz — they call logically tidy and untradeable as one ticket because Niño also soaks some basins. Traders do not watch one ENSO index. They stack agency outlooks, then farm-level 7/30/90-day rain and max temp. Deere and ADM sit on equipment and crush, not on a weather emoji.

## Mental Model · *Crop Calendar × Stock Cushion × Five-Factor Price*

> **Components**
>
> Map climate onto the growing calendar, not onto a headline. Five factors set the price; weather is one. Stocks and exporter concentration decide whether a 1–2% miss is noise or a premium. Goldman +24% / wheat +41% is the 2026 tape, with Hormuz and the Black Sea already in the residual. Fertilizer-via-gas is a long chain. Tools are Niño 3.4 plus regional rain, not a single blog index. Keep the method weekly: name the bottleneck (bandwidth, exam hours, token bill, trust, weather window) and only then pick the ticker. Numbers on this tape beat category slides. If the guest cannot price the unit — tokens, tasks, hectares, or a 40-yuan checkout — the mark is a story, not a position.
>
> **Application**
>
> ADM and DE are crush and iron, not a Niño ETF. MOS is the nitrogen leak, not a hydro story. Do not buy a basket because an agency said 'super El Niño.' Diligence stocks and who exports. A 1–2% global miss is noise when warehouses are full and five countries can ship. It is a premium when two exporters and a canal close at once. That is the whole episode. Niño 3.4 without a stock number is a weather blog.
