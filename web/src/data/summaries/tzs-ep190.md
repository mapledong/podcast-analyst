## Background

> Ep.190 is a Knowledge Planet quarterly session where Yongqing walks members through how the team actually uses AI inside the full research loop he sketched on ep.188 (idea → screen → deep cover → field check → track). This year's theme keyword is AI-assisted investing.
>
> He treats AI as staff that can simulate investment-committee voices — your view, masters' views, and market views — if you feed curated corpora (principles, interviews, tweets, Knowledge Planet dossiers) rather than one-line prompts. Modules are built like Lego: idea sources, ability-circle screens, strategy scanners (buybacks, high yield), research prompts, and tracking digests each need explicit requirements before the machine helps.

## Key Facts

> **F1** Idea layer with AI tasks: weekly tracking of domestic and overseas investment masters (podcasts, 13F-style holdings, strategy-aware labels); ability-circle discovery across consumption, internet, manufacturing, cyclicals with explicit A/B/C rules (e.g. cyclicals: historically cheap PB, industry/price near bottom, limited new capacity; tight supply + new demand; involution ending signals); plus strategy screens such as large buybacks vs market cap and high-dividend lists.

> **F2** Research layer: simulate masters (e.g. Dalio-style agents) only after loading principles and primary sources — not 'evaluate like X' one-liners. Combine self / master / market takes into an IC-like readout; reuse Practical Investments Knowledge Planet libraries (management evaluation frameworks, growth-stock checklists such as optical modules) as RAG context. Prefer high-quality info sources; generic web search mixes noise.

> **F3** Tracking layer and limits: AI drafts weekend/weekly digests across multi-dimension watchlists, flags names to research vs risks in holdings, and can auto-expand info sources by learning new WeChat accounts/sites/experts from ingested articles. Strong at存量整理 and tracking; weak at 调研. Web digging tops out (≈50 ordinary / ≈70 with tools / ≈85 elite scraping); important facts often live off-web with high-cognition contacts. Do not heavyweight unfamiliar companies before ≈1.5M-character coverage.

## Key Insights

> **1.** AI is research staff for存量 and tracking — not a substitute for 调研.
>
> **Q** Where does AI help most in the SOP?
>
> **A** Yongqing assigns it idea-source aggregation, rule-based screening, corpus-backed master simulation, and weekly digests that sync to the team. Field interviews and off-web cognition still require humans because web mining has a hard ceiling.

> **2.** One-line 'think like Buffett' prompts fail; corpora create the agent.
>
> **Q** How do you simulate masters usefully?
>
> **A** Load principles, interviews, tweets, and show libraries first so the agent is a living tracker of that mind. Then ask it to grade opportunities — growth optical modules, trader-style names, or management quality — alongside your own and market views.

> **3.** Coverage depth still gates position size.
>
> **Q** What stays non-negotiable after AI?
>
> **A** Unfamiliar names should not be oversized before roughly 1.5M characters of material — AI can accelerate reading plans but cannot license a 50% position on thin files. Core job remains clarifying hard questions with high-quality people.
