## Key Facts

> **F1** Positioning: Ant Lingbo builds robot-native foundation models (VA series; interview centers VA2.0), stressing control/embodied bases trained from random initialization rather than only upper-layer fine-tunes. Versus π's open brain narrative, Lingbo is a domestic brain-first industrial sample backed by Ant incubation.

> **F2** Core call: industry-wide the brain may currently lag the body; body designs do not generalize — the generalizable layer is the model. If the view that robots must own their own models holds, data must support that model — data scale-up (not rare handcrafted demos) is the bottleneck.

> **F3** Stack and timing: VA2.0 discussion covers native MoE, causal attention, and related design bets. Against NLP's GPT-1→3.5 cadence, whether robotics has an analogous GPT-1 / 'ChatGPT moment' stays open — the show pairs 'native architecture' with a next bet on 'data-native'.

## Mental Model · *Brain-First × Non-Generalizable Bodies × Data Must Carry the Model*

> **Components**
>
> Admit hardware can 'move' while brain capability lags. Pursue cross-embodiment generalization via a self-owned base model rather than skin-deep hardware stacks. The data flywheel must match model ambition; native architecture (MoE/causal attention) is method — scale is the decider.
>
> **Application**
>

## Key Insights

> **1.** The brain lags the body — counterintuitive, agenda-setting.
>
> **Q** What are China brain-first labs betting?
>
> **A** Making hardware move is relatively easy; generalizable intelligence remains scarce. Starting from the brain then filling the body flips the 'China only does bodies' story and explains why Lingbo treats the base model as the product.

> **2.** Own the model → own data that can carry it.
>
> **Q** Is the bottleneck rare demos or data?
>
> **A** Shen is explicit: if the view holds, data must support a self-owned model; scaling data well matters more than hoarding rare hardware.

> **3.** The robot GPT-1 moment is still unpriced.
>
> **Q** What does VA2.0 mean?
>
> **A** Native MoE + causal attention are engineering choices on the path to a robot GPT; whether the bar analogous to GPT-1 is cleared stays open — the next jump may be data-native.
