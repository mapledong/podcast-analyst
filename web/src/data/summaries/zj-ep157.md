## Key Facts

> **F1** Three stages, three market sizes. After the models arrived, Cai walks the products as stages. Chatbots fetched information. Task tools, Manus and Claude Code on the tape, finish a job. A personal agent is the next stage, and it is a service, not a piece of software sitting on a desktop. Put that on China and he sizes them at about 100 billion yuan, 1 trillion yuan, and 10 trillion yuan. Information, software, services. The three things a personal agent has to get right, in his summary, are intelligence, supply, and safety that is actually usable. Safety, privacy, and compliance are not a footnote. He says a large share of the work is still there, including authorization. Claude and Manus, in his telling, did not have to clear the same domestic permission stack. A personal agent that books, pays, and moves money does.

> **F2** Supply is the unglamorous half. A good model can know that a taxi request should call a taxi API and a meal request should call a delivery API. That is not the same as the ride feeling smooth. Each partner has a different interface. The product and engineering teams still tune the important scenes one by one. Ant's advantage is that many of those partners are already Alipay or Ant partners, so the conversation is cheaper than a cold start. 2025 they chose to connect the Alipay service graph first. He is clear they have not finished the permissions. Connectors, the same idea he sees in other personal-agent products, are how context gets in without asking a new user to invent the right question. Most people, especially in China, will not keep a long interview going with a model. A small group will. The rest need a low-cost way to hand context over. Personalized encyclopedia answers are not where that context pays. A personal agent that is supposed to surprise you only works if it knows you.

> **F3** The person and the subsidy question. Cai spent 10 years in the US and did computer vision at Google in the deep-learning years. He came back in 2019, started companies twice, looked at early investments, and joined Ant in January 2024 as the models were already up and Ant was turning toward AI. From late 2022 to 2024 in the Valley, and from 2024 to now in China, he calls the cultures different. The Valley pushes the ceiling of intelligence. Domestic companies ask what the same technique does in a product. Lingguang was scoped in March 2025. An internal version circulated in July. The external push was aimed at October and November, and he also describes the app as having launched in November, with 2.0 now out. Zhang asks whether this Spring Festival turns into a personal-agent subsidy war. Cai says his boss had a line for that market: in China's kind of competition, if Sam Altman showed up, he would be handing out red packets too. The agent that can spend is also the agent that can be subsidized.

## Mental Model · *Intelligence × Supply × A Service Market*

> **Components**
>
> Chat is information, about 100 billion yuan in his China sizing. Task tools are software, about 1 trillion. A personal agent is services, about 10 trillion. Intelligence is necessary and not sufficient. Supply is a partner API tuned scene by scene, starting with Alipay. Safety and permission are a large share of the calendar once the agent can pay. Most users will not build the context in chat. Connectors have to. The Valley pushes the ceiling. China pushes the same model into a service graph, and the boss expects red packets if the category is real.
>
> **Application**
>
> Private:Ant is Lingguang plus the Alipay graph, scoped March 2025 and out by November. Private:Manus is the task stage he places under the service stage, not the end state. Private:Anthropic is Claude Code on the same task shelf. Do not mark GOOGL off a ten-year vision job. Underwrite whether the taxi that the model knows how to call is a ride the user will finish.

## Key Insights

> **1.** The big market is services, not another chatbot.
>
> **Q** How does he size China?
>
> **A** Information about 100 billion yuan, software about 1 trillion, personal-agent services about 10 trillion. Manus and Claude Code sit in the middle stage.

> **2.** Knowing the API is not the ride.
>
> **Q** Why is supply the hard part?
>
> **A** The model can call a taxi. Each partner interface is different, so the important scenes are still tuned one by one. Ant starts with partners it already has.

> **3.** A real category in China gets a red packet.
>
> **Q** Will Spring Festival be a subsidy war?
>
> **A** His boss said that in this market even Sam Altman would hand out red packets. The agent that can pay is also the agent that can be subsidized.
