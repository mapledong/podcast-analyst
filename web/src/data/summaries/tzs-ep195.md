## Key Facts

> **F1** Xu Yang: joined Anta 2006 on brand/marketing, basketball GM 2013, ran Arc'teryx (石中阳/始祖鸟) 2019–22, then came home to the mothership. On his second tour: Super Anta (Decathlon-style), Anta Champion (≈2023), Anta SV ('作品级') — a Nanjing-West-Road store that does not look like Anta and is too small to matter. Official exit 2026-07-15, 'other arrangements,' long-term strategy unchanged. Trade press two months earlier said Super Anta would stop expanding. A-Fu's line: if this veteran could not raise the Anta-logo family, the rest of the building will not — including the next '安踏事举' reboot.

> **F2** Decathlon math vs Super Anta: Decathlon last year €16.8B sales (~¥130B), €910M net (~¥7B), 20.2% online, 1,902 stores, €7.05M/store-year (~¥54.4M) or about ¥4.53M/store-month. Super Anta, aiming at that box, was doing ¥500–600k/month. Anta once tried to sit next to Nike on price; sell-through falls once the discount is tighter than ≈20% because shoppers still see one tier down. Fila and Arc'teryx take the premium guest; you cannot paste that playbook on a mass 安心踏实 name. SV already folded back into the lifestyle bay, same pattern as 中国李宁 becoming a shop-in-shop. Fila's DTC win pushed wholesale district bosses out — they cannot run the new skill.

> **F3** Nike: Yongqing's open is taking online sales rights back from Taobo (滔搏) and Pou Sheng (宝盛/宝胜). A-Fu's cut of the last year: the global pile is about unchanged — not a disaster, just no growth. Four regions: North America +5% YoY, EMEA +3%, APAC + LatAm 0, Greater China −11%. So the book is fine except China. Three business lines get a split later on the tape; the actionable tell is HQ recentralizing China e-comm after the distributor era. Li Ning is the analog for a sub-brand that failed to stay a separate door and became a series inside the mothership store.

## Mental Model · *Logo Ceiling × Decathlon ASP Gap × China-Only Nike Hole*

> **Components**
>
> A mass Anta mark cannot trade like Nike above 80% of list, so the group needs Fila/Arc'teryx for the upper guest — and cannot copy those hits onto Super Anta / Champion / SV. Score Super Anta on Decathlon store-month math (¥4.5M vs ¥0.5–0.6M), not on the narrative. A veteran leaving on 7/15 is the tell that the main-brand project is stuck. Nike is a China problem (−11%) inside a flat-to-up rest-of-world book; taking Tmall back from Taobo/Pou Sheng is the control move. Wholesale people lose when DTC skill is copied across the group.
>
> **Application**
>
> 2020.HK is a Fila/Arc'teryx compounder with a stuck mothership — fade Super Anta/Champion/SV optionality. NKE: size the China −11% as the hole, not a global collapse; the online take-back is the 2026 fix. Do not treat 中国李宁-style door-in-door as a new brand. Pou Sheng/Taobo lose the e-comm rent if Nike keeps the keys.
