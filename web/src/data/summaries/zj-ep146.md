## Key Insights

> **1.** π-series is a public roadmap: capability, then generalization, then performance.
>
> **Q** What is Physical Intelligence shipping intellectually?
>
> **A** π0 establishes ability, π0.5 pushes generalization across tasks, π0.6 asks how to actually perform well once an agent can almost do things — Ke's RL group emphasizing simple methods with strong results. π0.7 post-dates the interview.

> **2.** Robotics needs the NLP-style end-to-end shift plus every adjacent craft.
>
> **Q** Why is progress so slow versus language models?
>
> **A** Robots are capital-intensive and historically siloed. Success requires vision data practices, LLM training experience, classical control precision, and hardware supply chains in one coalition — not motion algorithms alone.

> **3.** Self-building robots are the continuity milestone.
>
> **Q** What personally excites Ke beyond demos?
>
> **A** Humanoids are not the point for her — robots assembling themselves would mark species-like continuity through reproduction of the stack. Until then, useful real-world competence remains rarer than hardware that merely moves.
