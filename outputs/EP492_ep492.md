Topics: pricing, Series A scars, internal Shrek, and the JPMorgan SMB M&A push.

## Key Facts

> **F1** Product eras: two false starts (high-school merger-ratio app; college econometrics paper) then a real company at GPT-3, pre-ChatGPT — demos magic, product dead. o1 Pro was the first reliable 12-quarter search. Opus 4.5 from late 2025 into early 2026 could do junior banker work. An MD emails a deck markup and gets it back in 20 minutes instead of two days; that MD has not opened Excel in 20 years and used to spend three days on five pages. Bleeding edge is 10,000 agents arguing 24 hours because a PM will pay $50,000 for one idea. Models already feel smarter than anyone he spends time with; the work is plumbing. Core ICP is dealmakers (data rooms, SIMs, DDQs, CRM, LP updates), not public equities. He can still build a huge business in private-market infrastructure public exchanges already have.

> **F2** Moat and buyers: build perpendicular to Anthropic and OpenAI. Finance is niches with different data and MNPI trails a Delaware judge might discover. He thinks Rogo can get to $5B of revenue going deep; a lab walking from $100B to $1T will not stop for a penny to build a compliant data room. Pricing is per seat next to Bloomberg, FactSet, CapIQ, Pitchbook — AE plus SE on every deal — because token billing fights over whether $100,000 of tokens created $100,000 of value. Next are usage then outcomes (pay per SIM or idea). Customers are mostly large banks; Goldman TMT is the farm system. JPMorgan just said it will do more SMB M&A now that a banker can be a deal team of one. Rocket originates 40–50% of mortgages online; he wants KKR to test a sponsor-to-sponsor sale in five minutes, not five months. Auditability beats raw accuracy once agents execute.

> **F3** Firm and raise: John is the co-founder. More than 100 people came from Goldman, Citadel, Jefferies, Apollo, Ares, Blackstone and peers; they bought six fledgling finance-AI startups. Internal brain is Shrek; every conversation is recorded. Series A: David Tisch introed 40 names including Sequoia, Kleiner, Benchmark and Thrive; all passed. Product was wrong half the time. Keith Rabois closed a month later and called it Harvey-for-finance. Pat Grady said the next-year plan was not aggressive enough if every finance buyer decides in 18 months. Stengel will take 30% more failure odds to raise $100B-company odds by 20%. Jane Street took 15 years to own market-making; great investors have two to five years to fold a country of geniuses (Dario) into the life cycle. In ten years, 90% of EV at the best firms should sit in owned software, not people.

## Mental Model · *Plumbing over Chat × Seat then Outcome × 90/10 Flip*

> **Components**
>
> If you guessed the end state early, the first-mover product looked terrible until o1 Pro and Opus 4.5 arrived. The durable layer is last-mile finance plumbing: MNPI walls, data rooms, CRM, LP letters, agent-to-agent negotiation — work the labs will treat as a penny on the road from $100B to $1T. Price like Bloomberg until you can charge for a SIM or a good idea. Banks have the seats; public-equity TAM can wait. The shock is new for PE, hedge funds, and banks: AI-native attackers will not hold any workflow sacred. Put the latent mind of the best people into a system the firm owns, because in ten years that system is 90% of EV.
>
> **Application**
>
> Private:Rogo is the named vehicle: dealmaker ICP, six acqui-hires, 100-plus ex-finance staff, $5B-revenue path he contrasts with lab pennies. JPM is the named incumbent using the same math — SMB M&A that used to be too small to staff, now a deal team of one. GS is the farm-system and distribution print (TMT analyst class feeding the rest of finance). Do not buy Rogo as a public-markets chatbot. Do not assume OpenAI ships the exchange. Size bank software as the thing that keeps the franchise when people are no longer the whole firm.

## Key Insights

> **1.** Deal plumbing, not a stock chatbot.
>
> **Q** Why start in private markets?
>
> **A** Humans still coordinate the data room. Public markets already have exchanges. He can build a huge business before he hires the public-equity chef.

> **2.** Labs will not pick up the penny.
>
> **Q** How do you live next to Anthropic?
>
> **A** $5B of vertical revenue is a rounding error on a $100B-to-$1T path. They will not build MNPI rooms or agent auctions.

> **3.** People are 90% of EV until they are not.
>
> **Q** What should a firm start doing?
>
> **A** Copy the best people into owned software. JPMorgan SMB M&A is the early tell. Audit the agent the way you audit a bad analyst.
