## Background

> Yiwen explainer after a cluster of Chinese open-weight drops. Seven-stage model pipeline, license taxonomy (MIT, Apache 2.0, Modified MIT, Llama Community, Kimi K3 License), and how Alibaba/Zhipu/Qwen use free weights as a cloud on-ramp. Companion to a separate SV101 podcast on the same debate.

## Key Facts

> **F1** Pipeline they split into seven parts: data mix, architecture/params, infra (DeepSeek DeepGEMM; Kimi FlashKDA/MoonEP), training, post-train, eval, release. What usually stays closed: data recipe, mid-train tactics, internal toolchains. Meta: Llama 1–3 had real tech reports; Llama 4 a short blog. DeepSeek V3/R1 plus this wave's V4 Pro/Flash published MoE, MLA (KV-cache), FP8, and open-sourced DeepEP/FlashMLA — 'Open Weights plus.' Kimi K3 matches with a full report plus MoonEP, FlashKDA, AgentEnv for the three training jams: comms, compute, agent env.

> **F2** Licenses: DeepSeek moved from a house license to MIT on V3/R1. Qwen walked custom → Apache 2.0; Alibaba said it will open Qwen3.8-Max weights (flagship was API-only). Zhipu GLM-4-0414 onward MIT. Kimi K2 was Modified MIT; K3 is a new Kimi K3 License — free for most, but (1) >100M MAU or >$20M monthly revenue must brand 'Kimi K3', (2) model-hosting with >$20M trailing-12-month revenue needs a separate commercial deal. Llama Community License: extra grant above 700M MAU, past EU limits, no training other large models on outputs. Meta's 30B Muse Glimmer is Apache 2.0; Muse Spark 1.2 weights promised.

> **F3** Money and politics: free weights still pull API, private deploy, and cloud (Qwen as Alibaba's on-ramp). Infra names (NVIDIA, AMD, Baseten) sell more shovels when anyone can host. Palantir/Databricks get more surfaces. Jensen's first tweet rallies US open-weight; Altman and Pichai post support; Anthropic writes a long no — not a ban on open weights, but (1) frontier-weight safety, (2) distillation of US closed models as the real leak, which they say DC should police first. Critics read Anthropic as protecting a closed API. Jailbreaks at OpenAI/Anthropic plus future Fable-5-class local weights are the governance scare.

## Mental Model · *Weights ≠ Recipe × License as Moat × Shovel Demand*

> **Components**
>
> Score releases on whether infra and the real report shipped, not the word 'open.' Read licenses for MAU/revenue tripwires (Kimi, Llama). Follow money to cloud attach and GPU hours. Treat the US coalition minus Anthropic as industrial policy plus safety theater.
>
> **Application**
>
> Long the shovel layer (NVDA) when Chinese MIT-class weights spread. META's Apache muse-models are the tell if it is truly reopening. BABA/Qwen is a cloud funnel, not a charity. Anthropic's holdout is a product-risk if enterprises keep switching to Chinese open weights.

## Key Insights

> **1.** Open usually means weights, not the kitchen.
>
> **Q** What did DeepSeek/Kimi add?
>
> **A** Infra components and real reports (MLA, FP8, MoonEP) — Open Weights plus — while data recipes stay secret.

> **2.** The license is the business model.
>
> **Q** Who cannot free-ride?
>
> **A** Kimi's $20M hosting clause and Llama's 700M MAU gate are anti-cloud-free-ride. MIT/Apache is the Chinese default now.

> **3.** Jensen herded everyone but Anthropic.
>
> **Q** Why the coalition?
>
> **A** Chinese open-weight pace panicked the tape. Anthropic cites safety and distillation; the rest fear losing the stack to MIT-licensed China models.
