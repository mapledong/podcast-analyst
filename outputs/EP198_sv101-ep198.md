## Key Facts

> **F1** 2014–15 origin: Baidu Silicon Valley lab ($300M) trains a then-largest 300M-param LM in three months and writes the empirical scaling-law paper Dario later takes to OpenAI. Same year five SeaMicro/AMD engineers (Feldman, Lauterbach, James, Lie, Fricker) decide one wafer should be the chip — area ≈58× the largest GPU, on-die SRAM instead of HBM hops. Yield trick: 1–1.5% spare cores plus on-chip fabric, scaled from H100's 144/132 spare-core idea to a whole wafer. 2016–mid-2019 stealth: ≈$8M/month burn, ≈$300M over three years, 25kW on a dinner-plate, first customers 'maybe a dozen' CS-1s.

> **F2** Proof then scale: WSE-1 at Hot Chips 2019 (45k+ mm², 400k cores, 18GB on-die SRAM vs tens of MB on peer GPUs); $272M E round. DOE labs (Argonne COVID, Livermore fusion, NETL ≈500× vs their supercomputer). WSE-2 (2021, 7nm, 850k cores, 2.6T transistors); CS-2 trains a 20B model on one box (2022); CHM exhibit. Series F Nov 2021 at $4B. G42 Condor Galaxy 2023: >$1B, 4 exaflops, a 6.5-ft box built in 10 days — also the customer that blew up the first IPO attempt. Gen-3 sales 'in the tens of thousands' after gen-1 teens and gen-2 ≈300.

> **F3** 2024–26 product stack: chip → CS system → Andromeda (16× CS-2, 13.5M cores, >1 exaflop, 500kW) → Cerebras Inference (Aug 2024, OpenAI-API compatible, >2000 tok/s vs 200–300). Mar 2026 AWS: others do prefill, Cerebras does serial decode — every hyperscaler except NVIDIA. S-1 refiled Apr 17, 2026; lists May 14; IPO range $120–160, prints $200/$300/$380, closes $311 (+68%), $5.55B raised, session cap ≈$100B (≈25× Series C for the interviewed investor). Supply: all on TSMC 5nm including a 750MW OpenAI commitment, but they skip HBM, CoWoS, and 3nm.

## Mental Model · *Wafer-Scale × Skip the Three Bottlenecks × Decode Specialist*

> **Components**
>
> Put compute, memory, and fabric on one wafer so you do not buy HBM/CoWoS/3nm. Survive a three-year stealth burn with national-lab proof. Let one huge customer (G42) create scale and political risk. In the agent era, sell tokens/second via API and split prefill/decode with hyperscalers who are not NVIDIA.
>
> **Application**
>
> Treat Cerebras as an inference-speed vendor, not a CUDA replacement. TSMC 5nm allocation is the hidden share-of-market. NVDA remains the default train/prefill stack Feldman will not partner. OpenAI 750MW is both demand and concentration risk.

## Key Insights

> **1.** The hard problem was moving data, not FLOPs.
>
> **Q** Why a whole wafer?
>
> **A** HBM and multi-GPU hops dominate. 58× area and on-die SRAM make the transfer disappear — if you can hide ≈46 defects with spare cores.

> **2.** G42 both made and nearly unmade the IPO.
>
> **Q** What did one customer change?
>
> **A** >$1B Condor Galaxy turned lab science into commercial scale and then into a listing delay. Concentration is the point.

> **3.** Partner everyone except NVIDIA.
>
> **Q** How do they live next to CUDA?
>
> **A** Split prefill (parallel, others' chips) and decode (serial, wafer). Sell 2000+ tok/s agents, not a training monopoly.
