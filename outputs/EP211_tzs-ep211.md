## Key Facts

> **F1** Three books, one course. If a beginner and a professional have to share a shelf, Yongqing names 3 books by Columbia value teachers. First, Bruce Greenwald, Competition Demystified, the red-covered Chinese edition translated by Lin Anji and Fan Shuai. He has already recorded with Fan on this show. The book is how you tell whether a company has an advantage. Only then can you forecast. Without an advantage, the people inside the company cannot forecast either, because the world is too mixed. Second, Value Investing from Graham to Buffett, now in a second Chinese edition. It is the valuation shelf: asset, earnings, and growth. Third, the advanced guide for the securities analyst, retitled in Chinese. It is how you lay out the case for a boss, a client, or yourself, including the market and the multiple. Together they are business, value, and the memo. A good source points at a better source.

> **F2** What value meant in each year. He graduated in 2016 into private equity, domestic deals, placements and negotiated transfers, aiming to be an active holder around 10% and not a silent minor shareholder. Then a Hong Kong hedge fund. Himalaya Capital's Chang Jing lectured on value. From 2016 to 2019 the rooms were offline, then online, and in 2023 he moved to Shanghai for family. In 2024 he sat in on Gui Jiang's value course and did it again in 2026. Early practice was growth, with large swings. He came out wanting high-quality companies. The label moved without anyone drawing the map. In 2019, value meant quality. In 2021 it meant hyper-growth: Pinduoduo, the EV names including Li Auto, and new energy. Now the same words mean dividends, certainty, and a high payout. Ten years of practice is what made the split visible. The idea is easy. The strategy the market is discussing is not stable.

> **F3** Nerve, and other people's memos. After a drop he wants readers to be careful people, not louder people. Two books. The Courage to Be Disliked is the one whose title makes you quit, and the one he has kept for years. The investor trap is: I know this company, it should be 12 times, it is 10 times, the market is stupid. The book separates what you can do from the outside causes you cannot. That matches an inside scorecard. Liking a company does not obligate the price to agree. The second book is Li Guofei, Jobs, Zen, and Value Investing. Years ago it felt vague. Now he reads the psychology as the engine: resentment, invention, and actually wanting the problem solved are why someone spends the hours. He also says stock picking is not a place for novelty. If he wants an oil stock he rereads how Buffett bought PetroChina and Occidental. If he wants a cycle he stacks how the known buyers, including Deng Xiaofeng in gold, did that asset. The file is sorted memos from other investors, not a new theory.

## Mental Model · *Three Columbia Books × A Moving Word × An Inside Scorecard*

> **Components**
>
> Advantage first, or there is nothing to forecast. Then three ways to value: assets, earnings, growth. Then a memo a boss or a client can use. The word value meant quality in 2019, PDD and Li Auto in 2021, and dividends now. A 12 times business at 10 times is not a stupid market. The Courage to Be Disliked is the separation of what you control. Li Guofei is the claim that psychology is the engine. Oil and cycles are studied by rereading Buffett and Deng, not by inventing a method.
>
> **Application**
>
> PDD and LI are the 2021 meaning of value on this tape, not a current buy. OXY is the Occidental file he would reread before buying an oil stock. Do not treat a course list as a portfolio. The work is to have read the 3 Columbia books and to know which year of that definition you are using.

## Key Insights

> **1.** No advantage, no forecast.
>
> **Q** Why is Greenwald first?
>
> **A** Only a company with an advantage can be forecast, because otherwise the people inside it cannot forecast either. The book is the business test.

> **2.** Value is a word that changed jobs.
>
> **Q** What did the market mean?
>
> **A** 2019 was quality. 2021 was Pinduoduo, Li Auto, and new energy. Now it is dividends and certainty. The idea did not move. The strategy being discussed did.

> **3.** Ten times is not an insult.
>
> **Q** What is the nerve book for?
>
> **A** The Courage to Be Disliked. Knowing a company and wanting 12 times when it prints 10 is the trap. You do not control the outside cause.
