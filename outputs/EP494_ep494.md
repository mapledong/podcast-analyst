## Key Facts

> **F1** Energy and sleep: Elon is the apex of multi-company output and 4–5 hours; copying that is ridiculous. Huberman is great at 7 hours, groggy at 8, fine at 6, one night of 5 is survivable, two nights of 4-to-0 craters him. Grad school was 80–100 hour weeks; first four years of a company is don't die. Downshift: turn thoughts off via sensation, slow the heart with long exhales, forget limb position. An MIT group built an eye mask that measures REM directly — Whoop, Aura, and Eight Sleep do not — and stimulates extraocular muscles from behind the ears. He tried it; 7–12 months from release. Eight Sleep already changes temperature through the night to buy more REM or deep.

> **F2** Read/write race: anyone who thinks Meta, OpenAI, and Anthropic are only LLM companies will be surprised — every one becomes a biotech firm whose goal is to read and write the brain non-invasively. Eddie Chang (UCSF neurosurgery chair) maps speech in awake tumor cases and gets locked-in patients talking from planning areas, not the larynx. Neuralink's Matt McDougall sat in the lab; he and his wife have a receiver under the skin to open their door. Deisseroth ramped vagus stim on a suicidally depressed patient who, in the same conversation, felt able to apply for a job. A stimulator smaller than a penny goes under the neck; he guesses that within 12 months about 20% of the founder class will ask for one if it is only as invasive as a piercing. fMRI and EEG stay on the surface. Mayford and Tonegawa: activate tagged neurons in reverse, or all at once, and the animal still does the behavior — textbooks said sequence mattered.

> **F3** Blunt tools to specific ones, and the circus: GLP-1s bind hypothalamic appetite circuits and work when you ramp levels about a thousandfold; they also cut other drives, which is why Sam Altman talked about backing off. About 90% of adults drink caffeine. SSRIs helped clinical OCD and were over-prescribed for depression. He Jiankui announced CRISPR twins with the HIV receptor deleted; China punished him; Huberman has heard he now has a lab in Austin. Whole-body MRI, his medical friends tell him, will be commonplace in about 20 months. Public health talk is a clown show; easy to get attention, hard to keep respect. He expects a 2027–28 lull, then trained MDs and PhDs. Protocols is the book of things he already knows; waking states are next.

## Mental Model · *Sleep On-Ramp × Non-Invasive Write × Blunt-to-Specific*

> **Components**
>
> Know your sleep need; do not copy Elon's 4–5 hours. The skill that hard-chargers lack is shutting thoughts off, dropping heart rate, and losing proprioception — rocking, eye-mask stim, Eight Sleep temperature. Labs that look like LLM companies (Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic) plus Neuralink are in a race to read and write the nervous system without a craniotomy. Sleep is where the public will accept it first. Pharmacology and caffeine are blunt because receptors sit everywhere; the field is moving to spatial and temporal tags (virus plus hat, AND-gate pill, vagus under the skin). Memory sequence may not matter, which both helps and scares writers-to-brain. Health Twitter is theatrics; 2027–28 should thin the clowns.
>
> **Application**
>
> META is the named public that he says becomes a biotech company; Zuck is in his big four of brain athletes with Sam, Dario, and Elon. Private:Neuralink is the invasive pole (McDougall, locked-in speech, door implant) against the non-invasive hat/ultrasound path. Private:EightSleep is the product already writing to the nervous system via temperature and the contrast with Whoop/Aura, which do not measure REM. Do not treat GLP-1s as clean motivation drugs. Do not fade sleep hardware because the discourse looks kooky — he thinks the serious wave is next.
