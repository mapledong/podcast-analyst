## Key Facts

> **F1** Positioning: Guo answers 'I'm a long' on the AI wager. The fear is missing the opportunity versus repeating every boom-bust. Partner Mike maps ≈250 entrepreneurs/researchers who actually show up and push the frontier; the firm's job is to know and support them, not just take first meetings. An LP survey of who fancy LPs most want to invest with had Conviction #1 or #2. She rejects a two-lab monolith: high-agency people plus the right risk capital can change open-source and industrial-policy outcomes.

> **F2** Compute and product: major labs are so compute-intensive that a $750B spend plus thousands of people makes ownership feel thin. First-year hunt was technology-forward — workflows and professions that fit the models, which many LPs called nonsense versus customer-problem-first. Robotics example: she and partner Pranav met Tony Zhao and Chenxi at Sunday Robotics as Stanford PhDs after Toyota Research, DeepMind, and Tesla. Book also spans biology, defense, and law; she spends days-to-weeks grounding each domain. Open source is a policy and ecosystem question, not a slogan.

> **F3** Firm-building: she raised by saying there are things she imagines about culture, then 'we'll go execute like hell and figure it out' — hard for LPs, which she values. Parents taught integrity, independence of thought, and family/team focus. She starts at conviction and works backwards (Mike climbs to it). Debates inside the firm and a biology opportunity close the hour. Abundant intelligence is not inevitable if compute stays scarce and supply chains stay fragile.

## Mental Model · *No-Backtest Long × 250-Person Map × Compute Independence*

> **Components**
>
> You cannot backtest this cycle; the mistake is hitting the brakes at 90 mph or repeating every bust. Map the ≈250 people who actually push the frontier and stay close. Bet high-agency founders plus risk capital against a two-lab monolith. Treat $750B compute as the constraint that empties individual ownership. Hunt workflows the models already fit, not only customer-problem decks. Physical-world (Sunday Robotics) and biology are the non-obvious books. Raise on execute-like-hell, not a polished prior.
>
> **Application**
>
> Conviction is the private vehicle. NVDA remains the compute substrate of the $750B problem. Sunday Robotics is the humanoid/physical tell from this tape. Fade 'abundant intelligence is inevitable' if power, wafers, and policy stay tight. Do not wait for a backtest to size the AI book. Open-source industrial policy is a second-order bet, not a trading signal today.
