## Key Facts

> **F1** Positioning: SV event to project China's Physical AI voice and supply-chain links. Comparative advantage framing — China dense in ontology/supply/engineering + data fuel; not incapable of 'brains.' Early commercialization: self-hematopoiesis over pure research burn; ~¥150M large order referenced.

> **F2** GTM: Asia (JP/KR/SG) mirrors China SKUs (entertainment, edu, inspection dogs); Europe/Germany needs strong industrial distributors after ICE→EV supply-chain gloom. US mix of research labs (under-supplied), home, and industry via dealers. B-end cares about delivery stability, aftersales, generalization vs rule hacks — not 400k vs 200k sticker wars yet.

> **F3** Competition: extreme pace — RL loco leaps, clustered VLA/world-model drops, diligence and willpower as moats. Capital strategy: set China-#1 / Optimus-rival ambition then reverse-engineer 12–18 month funding buffers per milestone (compute, data, headcount). Domestic AI/embodied enthusiasm absorbs talent/capital even in soft macro.
