## Key Facts

> **F1** Method triad Xie stresses: don't invest what you don't understand; dissect what you do; prefer monopolistic/ scarce positions; always read ≈10 years of statements — one quarter is gambling. Graham's Intelligent Investor + Mr. Market must be internalized so price deviations become actionable versus stubborn intrinsic ranges.

> **F2** Loss patterns: (1) enter because prices rose / dinner tips / 30-minute AI chats — first wave profits then leveraged wipeouts; (2) endless chase of 0→0.1 tech novelty while under-allocating time to few companies that matter. Xie prefers specialist focus over 'complete oil' generalism — 40-year scoreboard favors focus.

> **F3** Chip overlay: GPU/CPU/memory/foundry/materials each have different competitive factors; today's era flips to seller's market — TSMC/SMIC allocate capacity. Case: ChangXin as China's listed memory scarcity vs CXMT DRAM lane — study one name deeply. Learning posture: daily incremental study; reject 'already know' or 'can never learn.' Macro: five applications booming reverse customer power.


---

## Mental Model · *Value Skeleton × Sector Anatomy × Focused Capacity*

> **Components**
>
> Keep Graham/Buffett as worldview; map the six semiconductor layers separately; spend scarce research hours on monopolistic bottlenecks; treat capacity allocation regimes as part of intrinsic value.
>
> **Application**
>
> Chip portfolios: underwrite 10-year ROIC/cycle history before AI narratives. Prefer scarce domestic bottlenecks you can actually diligence. Fade tip-driven momentum without process. SMIC/TSMC capacity politics are first-order, not footnotes.

## Key Insights

> **1.** Without ten-year statements you are not value investing.
>
> **Q** What replaces quarterly vibes?
>
> **A** Trend of margins, inventory, cash cycles, and valuation bands across a decade — then Mr. Market deviations.

> **2.** Chips need value tools plus sector physics.
>
> **Q** Can Buffett alone work?
>
> **A** Neither 'chips can't be valued' nor 'ignore foundry cycles' — combine.

> **3.** Focus is the edge after 40 years.
>
> **Q** Why not cover everything AI?
>
> **A** Generalists skim 0.1 tech; specialists know jin/liang on the few that compound.
