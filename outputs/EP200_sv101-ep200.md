## Key Facts

> **F1** Timing: Jeff Dean announced he would leave Google with other executives to start Discovery Loop, aimed at accelerating life-science and other research — recorded the day after the news. Cao reads three layers: (1) Gemini-era 'concentrate force' created internal conflict after years of separate teams; (2) last ≈6 months Gemini lagged — 3.1 Pro was still respectable early 2026, then 3.5/3.6 Flash looked flat while Anthropic, OpenAI, and Chinese open models jumped, and a 3.x Pro still unpublished; (3) Dean/Oriol/Sanjay/Quoc's Discovery Loop is the natural next step after math and coding — knowledge discovery / recursive self-improvement, a new Neolab. He rejects 'Google is over': chips, infra, data, talent, and consumer distribution are still in-house.

> **F2** Science scores on the tape: OpenAI Astra derived 10 math problems humans had left open a long time. An unreleased Anthropic research Claude pushed a Riemann-conjecture-related zero lower bound from 41.6% to 67.2%. Cao's bottleneck is verification — the model-to-experiment loop. Math has gone from proof-scarce to proof-surplus; a correct black-box result still needs human understanding. Conceptual abstraction is 'maybe AGI's last mile.' Nobel: at least 20–30 years. Unreasonable Labs is AI for knowledge creation, science, and R&D — same neighborhood as Discovery Loop.

> **F3** Org/product: after Gemini became the max project, Google had to centralize. Catching Anthropic-style coding-agent economics is still hard. Cao wants an outer mechanism that helps models generate different ideas (not just more samples). Human role is awkward once the loop works. Exploit vs explore / value function is the research-method talk; last-mile science is still conceptual, not more tokens.

## Mental Model · *Verification Loop × Proof Surplus × Full-Stack Google*

> **Components**
>
> AI-for-science value is the closed loop from model to experiment. Until verification breaks, you cannot iterate. Math already over-produces proofs humans cannot digest. Score labs on hard problems (Astra's 10; Riemann 41.6→67.2), not chat Elo. Treat Dean's Discovery Loop as the same bet as Unreasonable Labs, not as Google dying. Gemini concentration explains talent exits; the company still owns chip-to-distribution. Nobel timelines are decades because conceptual abstraction is the last mile.
>
> **Application**
>
> GOOGL is a priority/org story, not a 'Dean left so fade the stack' story. OpenAI/Anthropic science models are the capability prints. Private science startups (Unreasonable Labs, Discovery Loop) are the 2026–27 formation. Do not underwrite 'AI wins a Nobel this cycle.' Fade Gemini-only product takes while 3.x Pro is missing and Flash is flat.

## Key Insights

> **1.** Verification is the science bottleneck.
>
> **Q** What has to break?
>
> **A** The model-to-experiment loop. Once verification works, iteration is fast. Until then, more proofs just pile up.

> **2.** Google is not over because four people left.
>
> **Q** How should we read Dean's exit?
>
> **A** Gemini concentration plus a lagging 3.x line. The company still has chips, infra, data, and distribution. Discovery Loop is the next science bet, same neighborhood as Cao's startup.

> **3.** A black-box proof is only partly meaningful.
>
> **Q** What is the last mile?
>
> **A** Conceptual abstraction humans can judge. Cao puts a Nobel at 20–30 years, not this product cycle.
