## Key Facts

> **F1** Hongjun's open: buyers spend $1 on software and ≈$6 implementing it; that $6 is why every giant rushed 'implementation.' 1H26 tells: OpenAI stood up a deploy company; Anthropic a $1.5B JV with Blackstone and Goldman; Microsoft Frontier Company with $2.5B and 6,000 engineers, claiming to go beyond FDE; Amazon 'billions' into a similar org. OpenAI SF mid-level FDE base $160–280k, ≈60% above classic solutions engineers — Peng says the premium is scarcity of three stacked skills (AI sharpness, industry depth, data breadth), not a renamed staff-aug title.

> **F2** FDE versus resident ops: deliver a business result, not a feature. Three design rules — a measurable business goal (eval/metrics), enterprise data the agent can call without leaking, and a job-benchmark preset (best CS / best media buyer / best salesperson as a 5–10 year starter). The viral ops example: a customer who was promised a rice cooker and is missing a part is ≈260 steps across internal systems plus JD/Tmall and extra SKUs; that path covers ≥95% of cases. A manufacturing sales pilot turned the worst province into the best after an agent stitched pre/during/post (routing, leave-behinds, CRM writeback) that used to eat half the day plus two night hours.

> **F3** Product: 4+X — four preset agents (marketing, sales, CS, operations) plus X = that firm's data assets, approval limits, and capital permissions. Peng splits the world into +AI (old SaaS/tools get an AI user so humans stop logging in) and AI+ (rebuild CS/marketing/buying around where people and money already sit). Industry experience still has a barrier; the time to climb a 10-year desk may compress to 1–2 years. CSM/customer-success language is how he translates FDE for Chinese buyers.

> **Components**
>

>
> **Application**

## Key Insights

> **1.** The $6 is the market.
>
> **Q** Why did every giant stand up a deploy org in 1H26?
>
> **A** Software was never the P&L. Implementation was. AI is a fight over that six dollars, which is why OpenAI/Anthropic/MSFT/AMZN all hired an army.

> **2.** A late shipment is 260 steps.
>
> **Q** What makes Chinese FDE different from a chatbot?
>
> **A** JD/Tmall, spare parts, and internal tickets. Cover 95% of that path or you are still doing resident ops.

> **3.** 4+X, not a model drop.
>
> **Q** What does Lingyang actually sell?
>
> **A** Four best-on-the-desk agents plus the customer's data and money permissions. Without X the agent cannot go to work.
