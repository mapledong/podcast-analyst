## Key Facts

> **F1** HBM and the memory wall: compute doubles ~every 18 months vs memory bandwidth ~every 10 years — the 1994 'Hitting the Memory Wall' paper predicted systems would stall on data moves. HBM uses 3D stack + TSV + SK's MR-MUF mass-reflow underfill for bandwidth; thermal bumps can be ≈4× Samsung TC-NCF, making HBM a prerequisite for AI train/infer and agent workloads — swapping vendors requires joint re-qualification with NVDA and TSMC CoWoS.

> **F2** Competition and supply: 2026 Q1 SK hynix ≈58% HBM revenue share (down from ≈69%), Samsung/Micron ≈21% each; SK holds ≈60–70% of Rubin HBM4 orders. Each HBM wafer costs ≈3 conventional DRAM wafers; 2026 HBM output fully booked into 2027+; Chey cites >20% wafer gap possibly through 2030. Cheongju M15X H2 2026, Yongin from 2027, Indiana packaging H2 2028 — ≈$29B ADR raise expands capacity but meaningful supply lands ≈2028.

> **F3** Chaebol origins and capital events: Sun Kyung textiles acquired Korea Oil (1980) and Korea Mobile (1994); 1999 state-forced Hyundai–LG chip merger birthed Hynix; SK bought 21.1% in 2011 for ≈$3.05B. July 10, 2026 SKHY Nasdaq listing raised $26.5B, a foreign IPO record. Chey Tae-won vs Roh So-young divorce — a KRW 1.38T split reversed on supreme-court appeal — limited direct SK hynix impact but underscores political capital in SK's rise.

## Mental Model · *Memory Wall × Second-Place Bet × Storage Cycle*

> **Components**
>
> AI FLOPs outrun bandwidth — HBM is the shovel handle on NVDA's pickaxe; without it GPUs idle. Perennial #2 players (SK vs Samsung, AMD vs NVDA) chase edge tech and deep customer quals; MR-MUF one-shot molding beats Samsung TC-NCF layer-by-layer heat press on 12–16-stack heat and yield. Storage stays capex-heavy and cyclical — DRAM/NAND ASPs up 60–70% in places, yet memory has repeatedly re-rated down after 'this time is different' hype.
>
> **Application**
>
> Underwrite AI infra beyond GPUs: track HBM capacity, packaging yield, and second-source qualification. SKHY ADR adds liquidity and funds expansion, but supply lags ≈2 years; watch Samsung NVDA sign-off, Micron CHIPS onshoring, and 2028 supply-driven pricing resets.

## Key Insights

> **1.** HBM is the AI chokepoint.
>
> **Q** Why can't Nvidia swap vendors easily?
>
> **A** HBM needs joint validation on packaging, thermals, signal integrity and TSMC CoWoS — SK hynix's HBM3 MR-MUF heat path and yield won first H100 qualification, often the sole early supplier.

> **2.** Shortage runs for years.
>
> **Q** Why did Huang sign 'Please Make More'?
>
> **A** Agent infer demand plus each HBM die cannibalizes ≈3 DRAM wafers; Chey cites >20% wafer gap, orders into 2027+, customers offering to prepay EUV tools.

> **3.** Respect the cycle beneath 'this time is different.'
>
> **Q** How to read the ADR?
>
> **A** Markets price SK hynix as an AI infra toll booth, but memory has repeatedly re-rated down after super-cycle hype; HBM demand is real, yet share erosion and 2028 supply could compress margins.
