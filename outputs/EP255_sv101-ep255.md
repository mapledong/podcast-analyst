## Background

> Fireside E255. Second visit; March tape had Zhang preferring Claude Cowork to OpenClaw. Yiwen's question: leaderboards are near full marks, merchants do not feel it. Accio sits on Alibaba.com and 1688 — inbound to Amazon/Shopify/eBay or Taobao/JD/Douyin.

---

## Key Facts

> **F1** Two markets: coding agents are a solved penetration story (≈99% of engineers he sees). Cowork is copy and calc; commerce is long-horizon, multi-data, and the score arrives in a week or a year. That is why elevator ads in China are full of office agents and why Zhang still says the vertical is early. Claude Cowork made the category noisy; Accio stays on the merchant. The tape is dated 2026; treat every cap and percent as of the recording, not as a 2027 model.

> **F2** Product prints: search +20% overall, multimodal +130% — buyers now show the item, not just type it. Sourcing grew backward into market research and a design pack, then forward into supplier negotiation and close ('agentic trade'). Accio Work (April) tries to generalize those jobs. 107 tasks from live quotation-picking and shop work, run with no babysitter.

> **F3** Cost: same 107-task pass rate, Accio Work about one-third of Codex / Claude Code, because a post-trained small Qwen is close enough to a frontier top model on the easy slice. Harness plus context still beats a raw frontier call. Zhang: only an AI a merchant can afford is a useful AI. Amazon/Shopify/eBay are the outbound shelves, not the thesis — BABA is the supply graph the agent sits on.

## Mental Model · *Verifiable Coding × Unverifiable Commerce × Cheap Routing*

> **Components**
>
> Coding is 99% penetrated because Git compiles. Commerce is early because the grade takes a month. Stretch search into a design pack and a close. Measure on 107 real tasks, not MMLU. Route easy work to a small post-trained Qwen until the bill is one-third of Claude Code. Multimodal +130% is the buyer-behavior print. Keep the method weekly: name the bottleneck (bandwidth, exam hours, token bill, trust, weather window) and only then pick the ticker. Numbers on this tape beat category slides. If the guest cannot price the unit — tokens, tasks, hectares, or a 40-yuan checkout — the mark is a story, not a position.
>
> **Application**
>
> BABA is Accio plus 1688/Alibaba.com — the graph the agent walks. AMZN is an outbound shelf, not the product. OpenAI/Anthropic show up as the expensive compare Accio is trying to under-price. Diligence merchant willingness to pay, not Elo. 107 tasks are the unit, not Elo. If a merchant cannot run the same jobs next month at a lower token bill, the +130% multimodal print is a search feature, not an agent company. One-third of Claude Code is the only multiple Zhang offers.
