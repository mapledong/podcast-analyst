## Key Facts

> **F1** Lab culture split: Kimi feels tightest and product-native; Alibaba/Ant extremely 'corporate'; Zhipu/MiniMax run 'AGI loading' showrooms (42% progress jokes). Widespread 'tech ownership mindset' — super-app logic pushes self-built models (Meituan, Xiaomi, Ant Bailin) over API-only strategies; $500M–several billion per year on models still feels affordable. Structurally young researchers (17-year-old co-authors, 20-year-old public voices) and Tsinghua–startup overlap dominate.

> **F2** Open models vs compute chasm: After GLM 4.5/Kimi K2 in summer 2025, U.S. lost open-weights leadership; Lambert would pick Kimi and Zhipu as today's best open models 'at gunpoint.' China ships faster (RL finish → release within a day), compressing user-perceived gaps, but Meta/OpenAI aggregate GPUs still dwarf Chinese totals; one failed pretrain can erase a generation. Huawei viable for inference, not training; export controls accelerate domestic stacks but NVIDIA leads by years.

> **F3** Intangible know-how vs data gap: Frequent Chinese open releases build real-world usage knowledge (OpenRouter, Cursor picking Kimi via Fireworks). Closed U.S. labs enjoy consumer-data flywheels (Claude Code/Codex). Biggest surprise: almost no U.S.-style multi-billion-dollar external data vendor ecosystem (Scale/Mercor); weak RLHF/data-buying market for knowledge work. Many Chinese devs say 'we love Claude.' U.S. social AI-anxiety runs hotter; China is more pragmatic with direct compute anxiety.

Watch Huawei capacity to absorb inference currently routed to Western stacks, and whether China builds a data-labeling/RLHF vendor market.

## Key Insights

> **1.** China now leads open weights.
>
> **Q** What changed summer 2025?
>
> **A** GLM 4.5, Kimi K2, and peers flipped leadership. Meta slowed Llama; NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 Ultra is a rare U.S. response. Fast releases are China's pragmatic path to relevance.

> **2.** Compute is the overriding disadvantage.
>
> **Q** What's the biggest bottleneck?
>
> **A** Yes — compute. Rumors put ≈5,000 GPUs with a single OpenAI researcher, exceeding many Chinese open-lab budgets. Huawei handles inference, not training; scarcity narrows experimental breadth, forcing depth on known paths.

> **3.** China lacks data industry but has showrooms and ownership DNA.
>
> **Q** What leads / what's missing?
>
> **A** Frequent open releases accumulate how people actually use models; almost no Scale AI-scale external data budgets. Zhipu-style AGI showrooms vs Kimi's intimate product culture; megacaps prefer in-house models for super-apps and agents.
