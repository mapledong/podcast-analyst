## Key Facts

> **F1** Baker notes NVDA trading at its lowest forward PE in ≈10 years while 'the market 100% thinks they're significantly overvalued.' Visibility gap: public markets miss Anthropic/OpenAI and US open-source inference clouds monetizing tokens. Silica-style token indices dipped/flattened as mix shifted toward cheaper open models (GLM 5.2, Kimi) after a freak-out — not pure demand death; frontier tokens still carry debated 80–95% inference margins.

> **F2** Hyperscaler operating cash flow (MSFT/META/AMZN) is accelerating at scale; MSFT brought a huge June capacity slug that barely showed in Q2. CapEx-cut panic misread: they 'didn't cut CapEx'; SpaceX installed-base speculation and raise chatter muddy the signal. Meta CapEx telemetry hadn't shifted when freak-outs peaked. Open-source acceleration (incl. China models) is real but coexisted with continued US lab shipping.

> **F3** Long-horizon: solving continual/sample-efficient learning could discontinuity demand curves (train on far fewer tokens then learn in the wild) — SSI and new labs chasing August-era demos; Baker frames this as amazing for the world yet hard to price. Supply chain: breaking HBM LTAs risks share loss across ≥4 memory players + startups (Etched). China understanding is hard under decoupling; learning-by-doing cycles can't be teleported. Political risk: deep-red pro-growth states still need the industry to narrate benefits.
