## Key Facts

> **F1** TacticAI evidence gap: a model trained on 7,176 Liverpool corners predicts first touch and shots, yet post-2021–22 corner conversion stayed near ≈3% with no sustained lift; rollout timing undisclosed. Football resists AI because scoring is sparse and high-variance, outcomes hinge on rare events, opponents break patterns, and unquantifiable variables (reading the game, chemistry, locker-room politics) dominate; club-private biometric and injury data sit fragmented across thousands of teams with tiny samples.

> **F2** Football Tech reset: SciSports sold to VANAD in 2024 (≈$15.8M 2017 valuation vs 'next European unicorn' hype); StatsBomb→Hudl, Second Spectrum→Genius Sports (≈$200M), Sportlogiq→Teamworks. Top clubs have budget but are few; mid-tier clubs are many but thin on software spend — AI creates decision value but gets priced like SaaS, so point scout/video tools can't stand alone as $1B companies and must embed in video+data+coaching workflow platforms.

> **F3** 2026 World Cup tech leap: Lenovo as FIFA tech partner; 16 optical trackers per venue plus 500Hz in-ball IMU yield 150M+ tracking points per match (vs ≈600K last cycle); 1,248 player 3D scans enable 10 cm offside calls. FIFA AI Pro answers NL queries like Ronaldo heat maps; on July 3 Portugal vs Croatia an IMU caught Matanović's hair-touch offside — Spittle argues at high stakes you need 100% accuracy even if purists miss human-error drama.


---

## Mental Model · *Weak-Signal Sport × Data Fragmentation × Platform Consolidation*

> **Components**
>
> Split 'win more matches' AI from 'officiating/ops/viewing' AI — the former hits weak signals and unprovable causality, the latter scales with sensors and broadcast data. Match results swing on unobservable variables (weather, refs, psychology, locker-room politics); club software budgets are thin and transfer decisions mix data with politics. Point solutions get absorbed into Hudl/Genius/Teamworks-style sports OS; Palantir-style deep deployments fail because clients can't fund them.
>
> **Application**
>
> Diligence sports-AI startups on cheaper structured data from broadcast feeds, not championship promises. For GOOGL-class giants, TacticAI-like competitive AI is Nature-grade research brand, not P&L; event ops, officiating, and GenAI viewing tools scale. Treat Football Tech as vertical SaaS plus M&A rollup, not the next OpenAI.

## Key Insights

> **1.** No 'ChatGPT moment' for on-field football AI.
>
> **Q** Why did TacticAI underwhelm?
>
> **A** Corner conversion didn't materially rise after Nature; high randomness and unquantifiable variables block causal proof — clubs won't pay SaaS prices for unprovable decision value.

> **2.** Data is the deepest wall.
>
> **Q** Why no football BookCorpus?
>
> **A** Body-fat mapping, muscle-fiber mix, gait asymmetry, joint laxity live in thousands of club-private medical systems — small samples, no public access, and camera pose estimation can't match lab capture.

> **3.** AI changes officiating and viewing first.
>
> **Q** Where did 2026 World Cup AI land?
>
> **A** Semi-automated offside, 3D in/out calls, FIFA AI Pro Q&A; Spittle argues at high stakes you need 100% accuracy even if purists miss human-error drama.
