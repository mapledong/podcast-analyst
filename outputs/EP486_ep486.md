## Background

> Patrick returns to Benchmark GP Eric Vishria after Fireworks and Sierra became the firm's working telescopes on AI. The hour covers why hyperscaler inference looks slow next to a specialist, why the old AWS-kills-SaaS narrative failed, how CEOs should invert the 8-to-5 operating day, Cerebras as a cautionary compute board story, and why Hinton's 2016 radiology call still misleads unemployment forecasts.

---

## Key Facts

> **F1** Fireworks versus the big three plus neo-clouds: same stock open-source models and NVIDIA hardware, yet Vishria cites ≈5x visible speed and a multiple-x throughput gap that only shows in unit economics — specialists pay cloud margins and still make money, so running 2–4T-parameter models efficiently is the scarce skill, not access to weights.

> **F2** 2014–now enterprise history is his anti-zero-sum slide: investors feared AWS would offer the same software at ≈8% gross margin and erase 85% SaaS annuities. Instead Snowflake out-Amazoned Redshift on Amazon; Confluent, Elastic, Databricks, and Datadog (now a ≈$100B name) grew on the same cloud. He maps a 40/30/20 hyperscaler split plus Cloudflare as another $100B specialist — and expects AI to rhyme, including 'crazy' $100B smaller winners, if energy/power/shell/chips can scale.

> **F3** Operating inversion: hitting the plan is the bug. Anne Lee Skates's cut — CEOs running the old business 8 a.m.–5 p.m. and AI from 5–8 must flip it. He is dismissive of vibe-coding as the SaaS threat. Compute side: an unannounced new-CPU investment because LLMs emit code that still runs on baggage-laden x86; Cerebras 2019 board memory (chip 'melting,' ≈$500M in) makes him refuse more of that category. Hinton 2016 radiology: capability was right, Jevons demand for more imaging made the job-loss call wrong — same setup he sees in unemployment takes.

## Mental Model · *Specialist Inference × Anti-Zero-Sum Cloud × Plan Inversion*

> **Components**
>
> Treat serving efficiency as a company, not a cloud SKU. Read AI market structure through the 2014–2024 SaaS-on-AWS surprise (oligopoly plus $100B specialists). Force CEOs to put AI in daylight hours. Fade wafer-scale science projects without customers; watch energy before 'capital ran out.'
>
> **Application**
>
> Prefer inference specialists and app layers that can out-execute hyperscalers on the same NVIDIA stack. Do not short AMZN/SNOW solely because clouds can copy features. Size NVDA as the shared substrate. Treat 'we hit plan' SaaS prints as a yellow flag if AI work is after-hours only.

## Key Insights

> **1.** Hitting the plan now destroys equity value.
>
> **Q** What should CEOs invert?
>
> **A** Stop treating last year's roadmap as the job. Put AI in the 8-to-5 block and run the legacy P&L after hours — the old playbook compounds the wrong business.

> **2.** Same model, same NVIDIA, 5x gap.
>
> **Q** Why don't AWS/Azure/GCP just win inference?
>
> **A** Serving 2–4T-parameter models efficiently is hard. Fireworks' speed and hidden throughput show a specialist can pay cloud margins and still earn — weights are not the moat.

> **3.** The AWS-kills-SaaS call was the tell.
>
> **Q** Why will AI have room for many winners?
>
> **A** Snowflake, Databricks, and Datadog grew on Amazon. Expect a hyperscaler oligopoly plus Cloudflare-class $100B specialists — zero-sum thinking undersizes the market.
