## Background

> Matt Reustle breaks down AUTO1 Group with Harrison Moot of Sandstone. On the surface AUTO1 sounds like a European Carvana, but Moot stresses a different sequencing and market structure: founded in Berlin in 2012 by Christian Bertermann and Hakan Koç from the consumer-internet ecosystem, the company first built a multi-country "WeBuyYourCar" consumer-to-business sourcing engine, then a dealer marketplace (Auto1.com) in 2013, and only in 2020 launched AutoHero retail. Europe's used-car market runs ≈30–40 million transactions a year (~€600 billion) versus ≈10 million new cars, remains highly fragmented (top 20 dealers <10% share vs ≈20% in the U.S.), and rewards cross-border inventory routing—more than 60% of AUTO1-sourced cars sell outside the country of purchase. Financing is prime/near-prime under stricter European usury rules, not a Carvana-style subprime story. After a 2021 IPO and years of losses, 2024 was the first EBITDA-positive year; Kazoo exited in 2024, leaving AUTO1 as the scaled independent integrated player.

---

## Key Facts

> **F1** Europe hosts roughly 30–40 million used-car transactions a year (~€600 billion of volume) versus about 10 million new-car sales; the top 20 dealers hold under 10% share. AUTO1 operates in 30 countries with ≈850,000 cars toward a million-unit run-rate, yet still under 3% market share—management talks about a path toward ≈10%, with dealers only ≈15% penetrated of ≈200,000 European dealers and dealer counts still growing ≈23–25% year over year.

> **F2** In 2025, merchant/wholesale was ≈90% of units at ~€8,600 ASP and ~€700 GPU (≈12% gross margin) with ≈28–30 day turns (≈12× capital recycling; Sandstone estimates ≈5% EBITDA margin before overhead implies ≈60% ROC). AutoHero retail was ≈10% of units at ~€17,400 ASP and ~€2,100 GPU (≈15% margin) with ≈120-day turns; management targets ~€3,000 retail GPU. Twelve AutoHero refurb centers can handle ≈250,000 cars/year but run only ≈45–50% utilized.

> **F3** EBITDA turned positive in 2024 (≈1.5% margin) and reached ≈2.5% by 2025 against a long-term 5–9% IPO target. Financing is shifting from lead-gen to originating and securitizing prime loans (recent ~€250 million consumer ABS, ≈3.5× oversubscribed, ≈87 bps over Euribor). CEO L-TIP needs >€75 share price (from ~€20) and €700 million EBITDA by 2030. Sandstone sees ≈30% revenue growth near-term and >20% for years, with earnings compounding ≈35–50% if margins expand; stock ≈20× EBITDA less CapEx.

## Mental Model · *Cross-Border C2B Liquidity → Retail Attach*

> **Components**
>
> Source cars directly from consumers under local WeBuyYourCar brands; use a proprietary pricing engine (≈6 million transactions) and logistics to route inventory to the highest-value channel across 30 markets; monetize first through fast-turn wholesale GPU, then densify higher-GPU AutoHero retail and vertically integrated prime financing as utilization and brand consolidate.
>
> **Application**
>
> Under-3% share in a fragmented €600 billion market with no scaled independent peer after Kazoo's exit is the growth runway; margin expansion hinges on marketing efficiency, refurb utilization, financing attach, and centralized overhead—not on inventing a new category. Inventory mark-to-market and EV/ICE residual shocks are the real risks because AUTO1 owns the cars, though ≈30-day wholesale turns shrink the window versus U.S. retail-heavy models.

## Key Insights

> **1.** AUTO1 is a European liquidity network first, Carvana second.
>
> **Q** What makes AUTO1 different from U.S. used-car platforms?
>
> **A** It spent eight years building C2B supply and dealer liquidity across borders before AutoHero retail. More than 60% of sourced cars sell outside the purchase country—arbitrage classified sites cannot easily copy—while European lending is prime/near-prime, not subprime.

> **2.** Merchant economics fund the flywheel; retail is the margin lever.
>
> **Q** Where does the money get made today?
>
> **A** Wholesale is still ≈90% of units with ~€700 GPU and monthly turns; retail is ≈10% of units but ≈3× GPU. Hitting the €3,000 retail GPU target plus higher financing attach and 5–9% EBITDA is the path from ≈2.5% margins today.

> **3.** Competition exited; inventory and margins are the debates.
>
> **Q** What should investors underwrite?
>
> **A** After Kazoo left in 2024, Moot sees no effective scaled independent competitor. The live debates are margin potential, balance-sheet funding, inventory residual risk (including EV mix), and whether AI pricing helps or compresses spreads—not whether Europe needs this product.

## Chronology

> *AUTO1*
>
> **2012** Founded in Berlin by Christian Bertermann and Hakan Koç; launches C2B WeBuyYourCar sourcing.
>
> **2013** Launches Auto1.com dealer marketplace to match sourced supply with merchant demand and build pricing data.
>
> **2020** Launches AutoHero consumer retail eight years after founding — the Carvana-analog leg.
>
> **2021** IPOs near €38 (trades above €50 day one); management cites long-term 5–9% EBITDA margin target.
>
> **2022-2023** Capital markets tighten; rival Kazoo struggles and later exits (2024) while AUTO1 leans on wholesale liquidity.
>
> **2024** First year of EBITDA profitability (≈1.5% margins); continues refurb and retail build-out.
>
> **2025** EBITDA margins ≈2.5%; merchant ≈90% of units; prices second ~€250M consumer ABS; stock near €20 vs IPO highs.
