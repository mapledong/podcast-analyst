## Key Facts

> **F1** Market setup: Qualcomm stock +60% YoY into late-May highs as Physical AI and edge devices heat demand; Snapdragon pitched as edge-AI OS/platform. Xu: bring AI to every household — bottlenecks are model size on phones; answer with smaller task models + quantization; Qualcomm spans phone/PC/auto/robot/AR-VR.

> **F2** 6G angle: beyond faster bits — ISAC (integrated sensing and communications) builds radio-derived maps for robots/cars/drones (low-altitude economy tracking). Latency: push end-to-end toward single-digit/teen milliseconds; 5G TSN timestamping carries forward. Auto: ADAS + cabin AI + 'raise lobster' style compute demos; glasses (e.g. Tongyi S1 on AR1) for see-what-you-see streaming and episodic memory ('where are my keys').

> **F3** Robotics demos: partners like Accelerated Evolution K1 and Agaxi '通天晓' on Qualcomm Qinglong QCS8550 with on-device LLM/TTS for offline realtime interaction. Agentic phone vision: talk goals, phone orchestrates without hunting apps. AI PC defended via coding as strongest commercial AI use case.

## Key Insights

> **1.** Physical AI makes the modem company strategic again.
>
> **Q** Why Qualcomm now?
>
> **A** Edge devices need radios + NPUs + ecosystem software; cloud SOTA does not ship a phone robot stack.

> **2.** 6G is a sensor network story.
>
> **Q** What's new vs 5G?
>
> **A** ISAC maps and drone/airspace awareness — AI training/monitoring fuel, not just Mbps.

> **3.** On-device agents need ruthless model diets.
>
> **Q** How do phones run it?
>
> **A** Distill/quantize to smaller specialists; keep UX 'unfelt' while silicon does the orchestration.
