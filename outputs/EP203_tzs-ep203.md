## Key Facts

> **F1** Two oddities: price can leave BOM because the SKU is an expected face, and many lines repurchase inside a day. Lipstick still prints when the cycle is tight. Narrow four-block beauty ~¥400 billion; add oral, body, and adjacent and Xiao Z gets ~¥700 billion. 18–25 women: 20-plus purchase occasions. After 35, penetration ≈30% — the open slab. Households may keep half of a beauty budget in a familiar franchise and shop the other half. Baijiu is the 'never change the bottle' compare he says beauty cannot copy.

> **F2** Channel: the most ecommerce-native consumer category. One filing on the tape is 88% online / 12% offline — Douyin detonation, then wholesale and CS to rebalance margin. 80% versus 90% online is the street argument; he sits under 10% offline for that name. 618 and Singles' Day are the two prints they actually watch. Newness is the consumer want and the worst business if it never repeats. The job after sell-side is to watch an organization in a boom and a bust, not another P&L bridge.

> **F3** Names: Estee Lauder and L'Oreal are the prestige incumbents he pairs on the same expectation math. L'Oreal-plus-Nvidia is the one tech crossover on the tape — a beauty group buying compute, not a lipstick multiple on NVDA. Domestic names sit in the Douyin-to-CS channel story. Diligence who owns the 35–50 gap and who can take a Douyin SKU offline without wrecking mix. High gross margin is the start of the tape, not the end — fashion risk is the other half. Oral-beauty and body-care sit in the ¥700 billion fan pile, not the ¥400 billion four-block print. Households may keep half a beauty budget in a familiar franchise and shop the other half. 80% versus 90% online is the street argument; Xiao Z sits under 10% offline for the named mix. 618 and Singles' Day are the two calendars. ## Mental Model · *Expectation Price × Repurchase × Douyin-to-CS*

> **Components**
>
> Sell a future face, not a molecule. ¥400B narrow / ¥700B broad. 18–25 already repurchase; 35–50 is ≈30% penetrated. 88/12 online/offline is one brand's mix. Baijiu is the stable anti-model. Lipstick in a squeeze is the cycle print. The sell-side years taught Xiao Z to split a P&L; the company seat taught him that Douyin can print a SKU and still leave mix ugly if CS and dealers never catch the same bottle. 618 and Singles' Day are the two calendars that matter. 18–25 already repurchase hard; the open slab is 35–50 at ≈30% penetration, where a franchise can keep half the household budget if the other half still shops. Estee and L'Oreal sit on the prestige side of that expectation math; Nvidia is only the L'Oreal compute footnote. Fashion risk is why high gross margin is the start of the file, not the end.
>

## Key Insights

> **1.** Beauty is a priced feeling.
>
> **Q** Why can margins leave cost?
>
> **A** They sell an expected face. Lipstick still works when cash is tight. Baijiu is the opposite model.

> **2.** ¥400B narrow, ¥700B if you add the body.
>
> **Q** How big is China?
>
> **A** Four traditional blocks versus fan-beauty. 18–25 buy 20-plus times; 35–50 is only ≈30% in.

> **3.** Douyin then CS.
>
> **Q** What is the 88/12 print?
>
> **A** One name is 88% online. The move is to seed dealers and CS so margin is not all live-stream.
