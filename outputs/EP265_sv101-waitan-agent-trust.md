## Key Facts

> **F1** Who has paid: Han, last week, ≈40 yuan of healthy snacks on 阿福. Jorn uses AI to shop and will not let it charge — the rails are still e-commerce (user PAN or merchant subscription). Zhou is in the same 'decide with the model, pay yourself' camp. Liu used Xiaobu to collect Ant forest energy; Xiaobu GMV roughly 2x versus a few months ago, small base. Hongjun jokes she is the only 25% who has closed. The tape is dated 2026; treat every cap and percent as of the recording, not as a 2027 model.

> **F2** Chicken and egg: users want agents; merchants will not staff an agent SKU without users. Han says 2025Q4 everyone was bullish on agentic commerce and 2026 landed slower. Ant's catalyst: a wish wall plus incentives so developers build what users asked for. Mastercard's Superbloom language is the other growth metaphor. The fix is standards, not another chat model.

> **F3** Trust stack: Jorn compares it to the first time you typed a card into a website. Chargebacks trained the public. Now they need KYA (know your agent), intent confirm, and a global rulebook so a bad agent is the network's problem. He will publish KYA the same afternoon and says China already runs a more digital economy. Liu: the phone agent is an assistant; trust is the hard product. Zhou is still on the decision side of the funnel, not the settlement side.

> **Application**
>

Diligence who eats the fraud when the agent is wrong. Forty yuan and a 2x on Xiaobu do not make a GMV curve. KYA is the product because chargebacks trained the last generation to type a card.
