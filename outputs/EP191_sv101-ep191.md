## Key Facts

> **F1** Power symbiosis network: Blatter tolerated Americas confederation bosses like Webb skimming local broadcast/sponsor kickbacks in exchange for bloc votes — 'I let you get rich, you keep me in office.' All seven 2015 Zurich arrestees tied to Blatter's Americas machine; Coca-Cola, Visa, McDonald's ultimatums forced Blatter's resignation four days after re-election; Platini banned over ~CHF 2M 'disloyal payment' from Blatter.

> **F2** ISL original sin and the GOAL machine: Horst Dassler's 1982 ISL monopoly on World Cup rights created sell-high/kickback/buy-votes loops; a mis-posted CHF 1.5M bribe to FIFA's official account in 2001 — moved by secretary-general Blatter — proved the paper trail. Blatter's GOAL program disbursed >$2B in 'development aid' timed to election cycles; loyalty trumped integrity.

> **F3** Three-layer immunity and U.S. RICO: (1) Swiss nonprofit association — pre-2015 private commercial bribery wasn't even criminal; (2) non-interference charter with global ban threats (Greece 2006, Kuwait/Indonesia 2015); (3) 24 execs voting secretly on hosts and billion-dollar media packages. U.S. breakthrough: IRS Berryman on Blazer's 17 years of unfiled taxes, tracing wires via Fedwire/CHIPS; RICO framed FIFA officials as a criminal enterprise; Blazer wore a keychain recorder at London 2012. 40+ charged; Switzerland's 2016 'FIFA law' criminalized private bribery, but territorial limits still shield most non-resident execs.

## Key Insights

> **1.** FIFA corruption was rampant, systemic, and entrenched.
>
> **Q** Why untouchable for 20+ years?
>
> **A** Nonprofit shell + non-interference bans + secret 24-man exec committee = 'get-out-of-jail-free card.' Small associations depend on development grants; Americas bosses trade votes for kickbacks. Mainstream sports media stay silent to keep credentials and tickets.

> **2.** Jennings was the British reporter who cracked the wall.
>
> **Q** Why did 2015 happen?
>
> **A** His 2002 public challenge to Blatter won insider 'deep throats'; ISL's mis-posted bribe plus ≈$100M payment ledger started the paper trail. FIFA sued him twice; FBI picked up via Steele and Jennings introductions.

> **3.** U.S. DOJ ended impunity via wires + RICO.
>
> **Q** How did America reach a Swiss HQ?
>
> **A** Fedwire/CHIPS run through the U.S.; long-arm jurisdiction covers cross-border laundering. Blazer's tax evasion became the hook, then undercover recordings. RICO treats suited sports bureaucrats like mob enterprise members.
