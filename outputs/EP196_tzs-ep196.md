## Key Facts

> **F1** Definitions and guest: 2024 Jensen — next AI is Physical AI that understands physics and works beside you. CES 2026 — ChatGPT moment when machines understand, reason, and act. Xiao X: broker/sell-side, Tesla Optimus-class coverage from 2022-06, 100+ interviews from materials and actuators through mid-stack cerebellum/brain to body OEMs. Four types: industrial (pre-programmed, weakest generality) < cobot (vision, can move stations on one line) < humanoid (factory-wide gaps, more DOF, brain chip + actuator modules) < general embodied (multi-task; not all embodied is humanoid). One robot + two debug engineers cannot scale into thousands per plant.

> **F2** Bottlenecks: planetary roller screws — Tesla spec >95% transmission efficiency, industry often <85% on materials/process; usable on humanoids, not the main jam. Brain has two watchables: long-horizon programs and action reliability. Long-program pass rate is still <50%. Short runs can be 90%+ accurate / 95% 'cool' rate, then past three hours motor heat changes the sensor/control program so the same skill drops toward ≈80%. Driving still has not delivered L4 on four degrees of freedom; daily life and factory data are larger. A Zhiyuan-group data shop (whisper: 密封, likely 觅蜂) targets 10B hours by 2030 — about 20,000 human lifetimes — and even that may not generalize; some talk of 10 million people. Scaling law still holds if data can be standardized.

> **F3** Data and TAM: Ego Scale mixes human demonstration video, real-robot correction, and sim. Headset capture is the cheap standardized path — 20k hours already helped, so the chain wants 100k / 1M / 10M hours from housewives, factory workers, and SF Express riders wearing rigs at work. Volume: bipedal humanoids alone may not hit a million; wheeled plus toys could. Pure factory ToB tops out around a million units; 10M/year needs home (security, nanny, housekeeping). At 100k RMB/unit, 1M installed is ≈4% of China's 25M car year — auto-parts comps look rich unless you underwrite one-unit-per-year flow (4× the auto market). 2022–24 the tape says the chain was mispriced on incomplete information.

## Mental Model · *Physical-AI GPT Moment × Brain Pass Rate × Auto-TAM Ceiling*

Do not pay auto-parts multiples for a 1M-unit stock of 100k robots. Fade 'humanoid L5 before robotaxi L4' without a data-hour plan. Headset/Ego-scale vendors are the 2026–30 diligence, even if the name on tape is garbled.

## Key Insights

> **1.** The screw gap is not the killer.
>
> **Q** What is?
>
> **A** Tesla wants >95% planetary-roller efficiency; industry is <85%. Usable. Long-horizon brain pass <50% and three-hour heat fade are the jams.

> **2.** Generality is the ranking.
>
> **Q** Why four types?
>
> **A** Industrial is pre-programmed; cobots move on a line; humanoids cover factory gaps; embodied is multi-task. Landing order is the reverse of the story.

> **3.** 1M units is 4% of China auto.
>
> **Q** How should investors size?
>
> **A** 100k RMB × 1M ≈ 4% of 25M cars. Rich for a stock of parts unless volume is annual flow or homes open to 10M.
