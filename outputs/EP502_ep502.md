## Key Facts

> **F1** The business print. First revenue dollar September 20, 2024. A billion-dollar run rate on September 16, 2026, just under two years. More than 100 of the Fortune 500. About 500 employees. The early house ended crowded, about 70 people. The work shifted to enterprise. Use spread from routine migrations to everything, including at Datadog, Harvey, and OpenRouter. ROI, when customers measure it themselves, is about 8 to 15 times per dollar. One hour of Devin is about 8 hours of engineer-equivalent output. Citi has started a rollout to about 14,000 engineers. The firm has about 80,000 technical staff who build and maintain software, so Wu calls that a long way to go. Jane Fraser, on the earnings call the week before, said Citi expects about $1.5 billion of savings in 2026 just from bringing projects in house that used to go to consultants. That figure is before the new things those teams will build.

> **F2** Why security is the capability test. Wu released a security product the day of the tape. Attackers can train capable models of their own. The urgent gap is that defenders are not using agents at the same depth. A swarm has to walk every attack surface of the product and the site, keep it current, and watch logs and entry points. A lot of the code is about 30 years old and has to be brought forward. On the current path, the next 6 to 12 months produce many major breaches. Not small firms. Fortune 50, Fortune 100, and government institutions. He treats that as close to inevitable unless the defense swarm is deployed at the same intensity as the attack. The product is the same agent used to write software, pointed at the holes in that software.

> **F3** Compute and the engineering job. The title frame is cybersecurity, compute, and what engineering becomes when an agent hour replaces most of a human hour. The Citi number is the tell: 14,000 of 80,000 is the start, and the $1.5 billion is consultant work pulled inside, not a claim that headcount goes to zero. Wu still describes a long rollout. The ROI range of 8 to 15 times is what customers calculate, not a Cognition slide he asks you to trust without their own measurement. Fortune 500 penetration above 100 logos is the distribution. The breach forecast is the reason the security product exists in the same quarter as the billion-dollar run rate.
